Straight answers

What should you look for in a marketing agency in Wilmington, NC?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Wilmington, working with businesses that serve two cities at once: the people who live here all year and the visitors who arrive every summer. We run strategy, brand, web, SEO, paid media, social, creators and email as one plan, built for both.

Two audiences, one city

Wilmington asks something unusual of its businesses. There is the year-round city: the port and the riverfront downtown, the hospital system, the university, and a fast-growing population of retirees and remote workers choosing the Cape Fear coast on purpose. Then there is the summer city: Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach filling with visitors who spend like it is their job. Most local businesses need both audiences and market to only one, usually whichever arrived first. An agency here has to run two calendars at once without letting either one cannibalize the other.

The full mix, under one roof

Strategy first: which audience, which offer, which season carries the year. Then the channels, working as one plan rather than a pile of vendors. Branding and creative that can hold a historic downtown and a beach town in the same breath. Web design built to convert on phones. SEO and local listings for the searches newcomers and visitors actually make. Paid search and paid social aimed by neighborhood and by intent. Social media, influencer and creator marketing for a city that photographs well. Content and email that turn first-time visitors into regulars and regulars into referrers. Fractional CMO leadership sits over the whole thing when a company needs a senior owner for the plan. Our services page sets out the studio in full.

Built for a city growing into itself

Growth is Wilmington’s blessing and its squeeze: every month brings new customers and new competitors, and the businesses that were findable by reputation alone are suddenly in a contest. We work the way we advise: senior people only, the plan in writing, reporting against revenue, bookings and appointments instead of activity. You will know what is running and what it produced. A look at the work shows what that standard produces, including the products we build and market ourselves.

Key takeaways

  • Wilmington businesses serve two cities at once: the people who live here and the people who visit. Market to both, on two calendars.
  • In a fast-growing city, your next customer has never heard of you. Search and reviews are their word of mouth.
  • Judge a Wilmington agency on revenue and regulars, not on reach.

Asked often.

Do you work with businesses at the beaches, or just in Wilmington?

Both. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach run on a seasonal rhythm; Wilmington itself is year-round. We plan them as one market with two calendars, because that is how customers actually move between the riverfront and the beach.

We mostly get customers from word of mouth. Why change?

Word of mouth still works here, but the city keeps adding people who have no local word of mouth to hear. Newcomers choose dentists, builders, restaurants and realtors from search results and reviews. If you are not in that moment, you are invisible to the fastest-growing part of your market.

How do we start?

In writing. Send a note through our contact page saying what you run and who you need to reach. You will get a specific reply: whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost.
