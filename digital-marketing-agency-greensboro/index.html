Straight answers

What should a digital marketing agency in Greensboro actually deliver?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Greensboro, North Carolina, and our digital practice runs search, paid media, social, email and web as one system with one set of numbers. Triad companies do not need more dashboards. They need the channels pointed at the same customers and reporting tied to revenue.

The channels, run as one system

Search, paid search, paid social, organic social, email and web design are one system, not five line items: the ad promises what the landing page proves, the content answers what the sales team keeps hearing, and everything reports against the same numbers. Our Grow practice runs that system with senior people, which matters more than any single channel trick.

Digital for how the Triad buys

The Triad buys with its eyes open. Business buyers research suppliers online long before a sales call. Warehouse and logistics employers compete for the same workers, and those workers judge companies by what they find on their phones. Furniture buyers research for months around Market. A digital program here has to serve all of that — B2B credibility, recruiting and consumer demand — which is why we publish our answers to the common questions in the open, on our Straight Answers pages, before asking anyone to buy anything.

Reporting a practical owner will read

One plain monthly read: where customers and applicants came from, what each channel produced, what stops, what scales. Tied to revenue and filled roles, not impressions. If the report does not end in a decision, it is decoration, and decoration is the first thing we cut.

Key takeaways

  • Digital works when every channel shares one plan and one set of numbers.
  • In the Triad, digital is B2B too: buyers, recruits and trade customers all research online first.
  • If a report does not end in a decision, it is decoration.

Asked often.

Which channels should a Greensboro company start with?

Start where your buyers already look. For most Triad companies that is search — for products, suppliers and jobs — with paid media to accelerate and email and social to stay in front of people between searches. The order is a diagnosis, not a package.

Can digital marketing really work for manufacturers and logistics firms?

Yes, because your buyers and your recruits are already online, researching. A manufacturer whose site answers capability and capacity questions clearly will out-sell a competitor with a brochure site and a prayer, and the same content fills the hiring pipeline.
