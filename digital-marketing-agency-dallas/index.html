Straight answers

What should a digital marketing agency in Dallas actually run for you?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Dallas that runs digital as one system across the whole metro. SEO, paid search, paid social, email, web and analytics get planned together, suburb by suburb and corridor by corridor, and judged against revenue instead of impressions. One team, one dashboard, no telephone game.

One system, not six subscriptions

The usual DFW setup is a patchwork: a paid media buyer, an SEO retainer, a web firm from two redesigns ago, and reports that each claim victory while revenue stays flat. We run the digital stack as one system: search, paid, social, email and web planned against the same strategy and the same revenue line. When one channel moves, the others are adjusted by the same team that moved it, which is where the compounding actually comes from.

Built for the sprawl

Digital behavior in Dallas-Fort Worth is organized by drive time. People search by suburb, by neighborhood and by corridor — the Tollway, I-35, 75 — because in this metro the real question behind most searches is ‘how far is it, really?’ A digital program built for DFW targets geography the way residents live it: suburb-level search and paid structure, location pages that match real service areas, and budgets weighted toward where growth is actually landing, which this decade means the northern suburbs and Fort Worth’s edges as much as central Dallas.

Measured against revenue, not reports

Every platform will happily show you a chart going up and to the right. The only number that settles arguments is revenue. Our engagements report against revenue and pipeline, and they include the part most agencies skip: what got stopped this month, and why. In a market where competitors can outspend you in a quarter, the discipline of moving budget from what flatters to what performs is most of the job.

Key takeaways

  • In DFW, digital geography is drive time: suburb and corridor structure beats metro-wide settings.
  • Channels run by separate vendors optimize against each other; one system compounds.
  • The most valuable line in a report is what got stopped, and why.

Asked often.

Which digital channels should a Dallas company start with?

The ones closest to existing demand. For most companies here that is search first, organic and paid, because it captures people already looking across the suburbs you serve. Social, creators and email then build the audience search hasn’t reached yet.

Do you run campaigns suburb by suburb?

Yes, because that’s how the metro buys. Paid and organic structure follows real geography: the suburbs and corridors where your customers live and drive, not a single metro-wide setting that averages everything into mush.

How do we start?

In writing, through the contact page. Bring the site, the spend, or just the suspicion that the numbers don’t add up. We’ll tell you what we’d examine first and what we’d stop paying for.
