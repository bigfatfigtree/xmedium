Straight answers

What should you look for in a marketing agency in Charlottesville?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Charlottesville, working the way this small city demands: one message rarely fits, because the same business sells to locals, UVA students and alumni, and visitors in wine country at once. We bring senior strategy and a full studio to brands that punch above their size.

The full studio, sized for Charlottesville

The whole mix, from one senior team: strategy, digital marketing, SEO, paid search and paid social, social media, influencer and creator marketing, content, creative and branding, web design and email. Charlottesville businesses rarely need all of it at once — a winery needs booking-season search and a loyal list, a Downtown Mall restaurant needs reviews and a feed that looks like the food — but they need the pieces to agree, which is what a single team buys you. The full range is on our services page.

Four audiences, one small city

Charlottesville asks a business to speak four languages at once. Locals want to be treated as regulars, not tourists. Students arrive every fall as a brand-new market that has never heard of you. Alumni return with money, nostalgia and strong opinions. Visitors plan around harvest, football weekends and the Monticello Wine Trail. The UVA calendar quietly runs the whole local economy, and marketing that ignores its rhythm — move-in, football, graduation, summer quiet — spends money in the wrong weeks. We plan around the calendar the city actually lives on.

Word of mouth is the local algorithm

In a market this size, reputation travels at the speed of a grocery-store conversation, and a bad experience gets retold at a dinner party before it reaches a review site. That makes consistency a marketing channel: the same quality in the room, on the feed and on the search result, because Charlottesville compares notes. When a business outgrows its owner doing the marketing at midnight, our fractional CMO practice fills the leadership seat part-time, with the studio underneath it.

Key takeaways

  • Charlottesville is small. Your reputation arrives about five minutes before you do.
  • Locals, students, alumni, visitors: four audiences, four messages, one brand.
  • In a word-of-mouth town, consistency is a marketing channel.

Asked often.

Do you work with wineries, restaurants and tourism businesses?

Yes — hospitality is the local economy here, from Monticello AVA tasting rooms to Downtown Mall restaurants to the inns visitors book a season ahead. The work differs by business, but the pattern is shared: be findable when the trip is planned, be bookable without friction, and give people a reason to come back and bring friends.

Can a small business afford a full marketing agency?

Engagements come in shapes: a defined project, an ongoing growth arrangement, or fractional leadership, each priced in writing before work begins. The honest question is not the size of the business but the cost of the alternative — another season of marketing done in the margins of a job that is already full.

How do we start?

In writing. Send the problem through our contact page and you will get a specific reply: whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost. Nobody books a call on your calendar unless you ask for one.
