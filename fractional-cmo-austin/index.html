Straight answers

What does a fractional CMO in Austin do, and when do you need one?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Austin, and our fractional CMO practice puts a senior marketing executive inside your company part-time. Austin startups and scale-ups get strategy, a calendar and a team being led, without a full-time executive package. The seat is senior-only, never handed to a bench.

Why Austin companies rent the seat

Austin grows companies past founder-led marketing at unusual speed. A startup closes a round and suddenly has a board asking about pipeline. A healthcare or education employer adds hundreds of staff and a service line nobody has positioned. A scale-up hires its first marketing manager and gives her five jobs and no senior direction. The pattern is the same: real spend, plenty of activity, and nobody senior owning the strategy. A fractional CMO fills exactly that gap: an executive who has run the function before, inside your business part-time.

What the seat actually does

The week is concrete. Numbers first: where revenue came from, what each channel produced, what gets stopped. Then the calendar: launches, campaigns, and the agencies, freelancers and junior staff briefed and held to outcomes. Then translation: leadership hears plainly what marketing can do this quarter, what it cannot, and what the evidence says to fund. Behind the seat sits our senior-only studio, so the plan and the execution live in one company; how that differs from hiring an outside firm is laid out on our fractional CMO vs agency page.

The math, honestly

Published market ranges put US fractional CMO retainers typically at $3,000-$20,000 a month depending on cadence and scope, while a full-time CMO carries $300K-$570K a year in total loaded cost. Those are published ranges, not our price. Every engagement here is shaped in the first conversation and put in writing before work begins. The short version of the math: you pay for leadership days and skip the executive package, and the seat usually pays for itself by stopping spend nobody senior was questioning.

Key takeaways

  • Austin companies outgrow founder-led marketing fast; the gap is a senior seat, not more activity.
  • A fractional CMO owns strategy, calendar and vendors, and answers for outcomes in the leadership meeting.
  • Published ranges run $3,000 to $20,000 a month for fractional leadership, against $300K to $570K a year loaded for a full-time CMO.

Asked often.

How many days a week does a fractional CMO work?

Most engagements run one to three days a week equivalent, weighted toward launches and planning cycles. The shape is set against what actually needs leading: a company fresh off a funding round needs more seat time than one holding a steady course.

Does a fractional CMO replace our agency?

Not necessarily. Agencies execute; the CMO sets the strategy, writes the brief and judges the work. Many Austin companies keep their agencies and finally give them senior direction. At Extra Medium the seat and the studio sit in one company, which removes the hand-off entirely if you want it to.
