Client stories

The receipts
Growth

The consumer brand that needed a growth system, not more ads

A high-growth nutritional supplement brand had traction and a scattered set of channels. We took ownership of strategy end to end: investment across Google, Meta, affiliate, influencer, creator, UGC, Reddit and AI-driven channels; creator and affiliate programs built into real acquisition channels; AI across reporting, creative and workflow. Growth became a system with an owner.

Reinvention

The manufacturer that had to become one brand after an acquisition

A global radiation protection manufacturer needed global marketing strategy across website, CRM, automation, ecommerce, SEO, paid media and product marketing, plus post-acquisition brand integration. We built the digital foundation, a physician-facing podcast, KOL and education programs, and supported product launches and international expansion.

Scale

The Fortune 500 retailer whose websites and apps had to work harder

Leading planning, execution and optimization for the websites and apps of three retail banners — ecommerce, mobile, merchandising, loyalty and seasonal campaigns for millions of customers. Improvements to average order value, engagement, mobile installs and SEO from content strategy, technical optimization and continuous testing.

New revenue

The music company whose next chapter was enterprise

A subscription music-licensing business needed a new revenue model. We helped launch its enterprise licensing product, shaped go-to-market with product, engineering and sales leadership, and onboarded early enterprise adopters including Microsoft and Baylor University.

Launch

The platform we launched ourselves

Before Extra Medium, our founder created a digital music platform from idea to launch — raised seed funding, built the team, onboarded artists and led the marketing that grew it past 900,000 users.

Build

The B2B division that needed to exist

A contract manufacturer wanted to sell a new idea: helping major brands in hospitality, travel, food and beverage, fitness and retail create wellness products under their own names. We built the positioning and website for the division as the invisible engine behind other brands, with clear industry pages and a focused path to partnership.
