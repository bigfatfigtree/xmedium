Straight answers

Looking for an alternative to Embryo in Manchester?

Embryo is a specialist agency in Manchester — strong in its lane, and only its lane: growth channels run at scale. Extra Medium is the other model: a fractional CMO in your leadership meetings, a senior-only studio behind the seat, AI and platform builders in-house, and the price in writing before work begins. Channels or leadership: choose.

What Embryo does

Embryo is a growth agency on Portland Street in Manchester, part of The Embryo Group. Its services are SEO, digital PR, content marketing, paid social, PPC, affiliate marketing and creative, plus web design and development and growth strategy. Its PPC team manages over £2 million of Google ad spend a month. Published clients include the Inn Collection Group, TOTUM, MysteryFootball, The Piece Hall, I SAW IT FIRST and Native Communities.

Where Extra Medium is different

Extra Medium does not sell channel execution by the department. You hire a leadership seat: a named, founder-led senior operator in your meetings who owns positioning, the plan and the budget. That model is set out on our fractional CMO in Manchester page.

Behind the seat, one studio executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR work, so the strategy and the making stay under one roof. The full picture is on our marketing agency in Manchester page. The engagement shape and price are put in writing before work begins.

That is the trade-off built into the department model, whoever runs it. Each department optimises its own channel, not your whole business: the PPC can be excellent while positioning and product drift untouched. Departments also mean layers between you and the people doing the work, with strategy in one silo and execution in another. Extra Medium is built the other way round: one senior operator owns the whole plan, the studio behind the seat does the work directly, and every channel answers to the same strategy.

The part most agencies cannot do

Then there is the building. Extra Medium runs a lab that ships products, not just campaigns: a live creator marketplace (Magpie) and a live platform for ticketed celebrity sessions (Hapsta) were both designed, built and launched by this team. If growth needs a platform under it, a product, an app, a tool your customers log into, you do not have to hire a second firm and teach it your business.

Its Intelligence practice does the same on the AI side: getting companies cited by AI search, building research and content systems, and deploying AI assistants with a hard privacy rule, a company’s data answers only its own questions. The operator in the seat has run digital strategy for media companies and influencer marketing at scale for large brands, so the advice comes from having shipped it.

Who should hire which

Hire Embryo if you want their lane run hard by a specialist team and you will supply the strategy above it yourself: SEO, PPC, paid social and digital PR at serious spend, executed by channel departments while your own leadership sets the direction. That suits a business whose strategy is settled and whose only gap is channel firepower.

Hire Extra Medium if the gap is the strategy itself. You get a fractional CMO in your leadership meetings, senior-only people on the work, the price in writing before anything begins, and AI expertise and platform builders in-house when the plan needs them, one team owning strategy and execution together. The first conversation is free and specific, so start there and judge the difference for yourself.

Asked often.

Is Extra Medium a good alternative to Embryo?

Yes, if the gap is leadership rather than channel capacity. Three contrasts decide it. First, ownership: Embryo's departments run channels; Extra Medium's fractional CMO owns positioning, plan and budget across the whole business. Second, seniority: you deal with a named, founder-led senior operator in your meetings, not layers of account handling. Third, range and clarity: a studio, an AI practice and a platform lab sit behind the seat, and the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins, while Embryo publishes no pricing. If your strategy is settled and you only need channels run harder, Embryo fits; if nobody senior owns the plan, it is a different purchase.

Does Embryo publish its pricing?

No. Embryo does not publish pricing on its website, checked in November 2026, so its fees emerge through a proposal process. Extra Medium does not publish a rate card either; instead, once the seat has been scoped to your organisation, the engagement shape and price are put in writing before work begins.
