What Embryo does

Embryo is a Manchester growth agency based on Portland Street, describing itself as a growth partner for ambitious businesses, under the line "Transforming potential. Fuelling growth." Its PPC team manages over £2 million of Google ad spend a month, and the agency now sits within The Embryo Group, alongside content and creative businesses that have merged into it. It is also a member of AMIN Worldwide, an alliance of over 50 independent marketing agencies.

The services cover the main growth channels: SEO, digital PR, content marketing, paid social, PPC, affiliate marketing and creative, plus web design and development and growth strategy. Published case studies and testimonials name the Inn Collection Group, TOTUM, MysteryFootball, The Piece Hall, I SAW IT FIRST and Native Communities, with lead generation and consumer and e-commerce brands featuring heavily.

Where Extra Medium is different

Extra Medium does not sell channel execution by the department. You hire a leadership seat: a named, founder-led senior operator in your meetings who owns positioning, the plan and the budget. That model is set out on our fractional CMO in Manchester page.

Behind the seat, one studio executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR work, so the strategy and the making stay under one roof. The full picture is on our marketing agency in Manchester page. The engagement shape and price are put in writing before work begins.

The part most agencies cannot do

Then there is the building. Extra Medium runs a lab that ships products, not just campaigns: a live creator marketplace (Magpie) and a live platform for ticketed celebrity sessions (Hapsta) were both designed, built and launched by this team. If growth needs a platform under it, a product, an app, a tool your customers log into, you do not have to hire a second firm and teach it your business.

Its Intelligence practice does the same on the AI side: getting companies cited by AI search, building research and content systems, and deploying AI assistants with a hard privacy rule, a company’s data answers only its own questions. The operator in the seat has run digital strategy for media companies and influencer marketing at scale for large brands, so the advice comes from having shipped it.

Who should hire which

Hire Embryo if you want a growth agency running serious paid budgets and several channels at once: a team whose PPC operation alone manages over £2 million of Google spend a month, with SEO, digital PR and creative alongside it. Ambitious consumer, e-commerce and lead generation brands are the centre of its case studies.

Consider Extra Medium if the gap is a person, not a channel: nobody senior owns the strategy, suppliers pull in different directions, or you want one accountable leader in place before scaling spend. Channels work harder once somebody owns the plan they serve.
