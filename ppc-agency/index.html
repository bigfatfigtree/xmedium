Straight answers

Which digital marketing agency is best for PPC advertising?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency, and we run PPC the way auctions actually work: you are not buying clicks, you are bidding against competitors for a customer whose value you should already know. The best PPC agency is the one that starts with your margin and close rate, then builds the account around them.

You are bidding in an auction, not buying a channel

PPC is not a price list; it is an auction that runs every time someone searches. Your competitors set your costs, the platform rewards relevance and page quality with lower prices, and the same click can be a bargain for one business and a loss for another doing identical work. That means the bid is not a media decision — it is a business decision about what a customer is worth to you. An agency that starts inside the ad account, talking about settings and structure before it knows your numbers, is optimizing the auction while ignoring the arithmetic that decides whether winning it pays.

The account is not where campaigns are won

Three things outside the platform decide most PPC outcomes. The offer: something specific worth clicking, not a homepage and a hope. The page: it must continue the exact promise the ad made, in the same words, or the visitor leaves and the click is spent. The follow-up: interest bought at auction prices and answered tomorrow is money burned politely. PPC multiplies whatever is already true about your business, including the leaks — which is why, in a full lead system like the one on our lead generation agency page, the account is built last, not first.

How Extra Medium runs PPC

We start with the math: margin on a customer, close rate, and the most a lead may therefore cost. Then the account is built around those numbers — campaigns separated by intent, budgets pointed at the searches closest to buying, and reporting that ends at qualified leads and sales, not click-through rates presented as results. If the math says PPC cannot pay for your business yet, we say so and point at what to fix first; that honesty is part of how our studio services are run. When the math works, paid search is the fastest honest test of an offer a business can buy.

Key takeaways

  • Extra Medium starts PPC with your margin and close rate, not the ad account.
  • You are not buying clicks. You are bidding on customers.
  • Campaigns are won in the offer and the follow-up, then proven in the auction.

Asked often.

How much should we spend on PPC?

A test budget sized by your math: enough to buy real data on your cost per qualified lead, small enough that the lesson is affordable either way. Anyone naming a spend figure before asking your margin and close rate is guessing.

How fast should PPC show results?

Clicks arrive immediately; a verdict takes longer. Judge on qualified leads across a few weeks of consistent spend, not day-three revenue. Killing a sound campaign at week one measures the launch, not the channel.

How do we start with Extra Medium?

In writing, through the contact page. Bring your margin and close rate if you have them. The first conversation runs the auction math for your market and says plainly whether PPC can pay for you yet.
