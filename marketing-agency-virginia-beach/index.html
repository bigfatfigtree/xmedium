Straight answers

A marketing agency in Virginia Beach that works like an owner, not a vendor?

Extra Medium is a founder-led marketing agency headquartered in Virginia Beach, Virginia. It launches, reinvents and grows businesses — brand, creative, growth and technology under one roof — and it also builds its own products in Extra Medium Labs, led by Magpie. The practice is senior-only: the person you meet is the person who does the work, and clients range from pre-launch startups to Fortune 100 companies across the US and UK.

What the studio does

Six practices, one team. Launch: positioning, naming, identity and go-to-market as one arc. Reinvent: rebrands that fix the offer and story before the label. Grow: ongoing growth systems — content, campaigns, conversion. Lead: fractional CMO leadership, senior-only. Intelligence: AI consulting, agents and automation that survive contact with real workflows. Experiences: events and moments people actually remember.

Why Hampton Roads companies work with us

Local matters for some things: knowing the market, being in the room, understanding how business actually moves in Hampton Roads. For everything else, the work travels — most engagements run remotely with in-person time when it matters, and the client list runs from Virginia Beach to Chicago to London. What doesn’t change by geography is the standard: senior people, plain talk, and advice we test on our own products first.

How an engagement starts

One conversation. Bring the project, the bigger problem, or the marketing seat you need filled. The first conversation is free and specific — you will leave knowing whether we can help, what we’d do first, and what it would cost. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll say who might be.

Asked often.

Is Extra Medium only for Virginia Beach clients?

No. Virginia Beach is home and the first market for our own products, but studio clients are across the US and UK. Local knowledge is an advantage we bring to Hampton Roads work, not a boundary on the practice.

What size companies does Extra Medium work with?

From pre-launch startups to Fortune 100 companies. The common thread is not size; it is a founder or leadership team that wants senior marketing thinking without building a full department first.

Does Extra Medium take project work or only retainers?

Both. Engagements come in three shapes: fractional leadership, defined projects, and ongoing growth. The shape follows the problem, not the other way around.
