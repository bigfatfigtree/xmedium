Straight answers

Need a fractional CMO in Roanoke without a full-time executive salary?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Roanoke, Virginia, and its Lead practice puts a senior fractional CMO inside your company part-time. You get CMO-level strategy, vendor management and accountability for a fraction of a full-time executive hire — sized for Roanoke budgets, not Richmond or Charlotte ones.

What the seat actually does

A fractional CMO owns the marketing function the way a full-time CMO would, on a part-time cadence: diagnosis first, then one written strategy, then running the calendar, the budget and every vendor against it. In leadership meetings they translate — what marketing can deliver this quarter, what it cannot, and what the evidence says to fund. Our Lead practice is senior-only and founder-led, and the studio sits underneath it, so strategy and execution never live in different companies.

Why the model fits Roanoke

Roanoke’s economy is anchored by healthcare, with a growing biomedical and technology corridor, a serious outdoor-tourism trade and a long tail of professional firms and family businesses. Plenty of them have outgrown the founder doing marketing at midnight; almost none can justify a full-time chief marketing officer. The fractional model was built for exactly this gap, and the published market range reflects it: fractional CMO retainers typically run $3,000 to $20,000 a month depending on cadence and scope, a fraction of an executive package. Compare the shapes on our cost page before you talk to anyone, including us.

How it works here

Most engagements run one to three days a week equivalent, weighted toward planning cycles and launches. The start is a diagnosis and strategy phase, priced on its own, so both sides see the size of the job before committing to a cadence. Then the rhythm settles: numbers first, calendar second, vendors held to outcomes, and a strategy that keeps working on the days the CMO is not in the room.

Key takeaways

  • A fractional CMO gives Roanoke companies senior marketing leadership at a part-time price.
  • The seat owns strategy and vendors, so spend stops being a pile of disconnected invoices.
  • Published market range for fractional CMO retainers: typically $3,000 to $20,000 a month.

Asked often.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

Published market ranges put fractional CMO retainers at typically $3,000 to $20,000 a month, set by cadence and scope. That is a market range, not a quote: Extra Medium shapes each engagement in the first conversation and puts the number in writing before work begins.

Who is a fractional CMO right for in Roanoke?

Founder-led companies whose marketing has outgrown improvisation: practices and firms spending on agencies with nobody senior on their side of the table, tourism operators with real seasons to fill, and growing companies between marketing hires.
