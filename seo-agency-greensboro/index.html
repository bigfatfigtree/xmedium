Straight answers

How does an SEO agency in Greensboro get you found across the Triad?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Greensboro, North Carolina, and our SEO work treats the Triad as what it is: three cities that search like one market. We fix the technical base, build pages for what your customers actually type, and earn the local signals that decide who Google and the AI answers put first.

One market, three city names

People in Greensboro work in Winston-Salem, shop in High Point and search for all three interchangeably. Google knows it, and your SEO has to be built for it: one strong presence, content that genuinely serves each city’s searches, and a Google Business Profile kept accurate and reviewed. Our SEO practice builds that structure without the thin duplicate pages search engines discount — every page has to earn its place with real, distinct substance.

The technical base, and content that answers

Rankings rest on unglamorous work: a fast, well-structured site, pages that answer the exact questions buyers type, and local proof — reviews, listings, links from real Triad organizations. The same structure is what answer engines now quote, which is why we build pages like our Straight Answers: a direct answer up top, real substance underneath, questions answered in the open.

B2B SEO is the quiet opportunity

Everybody expects restaurants to fight over Google. Fewer people notice Triad manufacturers and logistics firms doing the same thing, quieter and for bigger contracts. Buyers research suppliers, capabilities and certifications online for weeks before calling anyone. The firm whose site answers those questions ranks, gets shortlisted, and often wins before the first meeting. In a region built on making and moving things, that is where SEO money is made.

Key takeaways

  • The Triad searches like one market. Your SEO should be built that way.
  • Rankings follow substance: technical health, real answers and local proof.
  • For Triad manufacturers, Google is the new trade-show floor. Buyers research long before they call.

Asked often.

How long does SEO take to pay off?

Technical and local fixes can show movement in weeks; durable ranking gains usually build over three to six months and compound. Paid search can bridge the gap while the asset builds. Anyone guaranteeing page one in a month is guaranteeing something they do not control.

Do we need separate pages for Greensboro, Winston-Salem and High Point?

Only where the page can carry real, city-specific substance — different customers, different proof, different questions. Three thin copies of the same page hurt more than one strong Triad page helps.
