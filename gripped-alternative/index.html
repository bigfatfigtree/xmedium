Straight answers

Looking for an alternative to Gripped in London?

Gripped is a London agency founded in 2017 for B2B SaaS, AI and tech companies, its 25-strong team running demand generation, paid media and SEO. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in London: a fractional CMO seat plus a studio under one roof, founder-led, senior-only, price in writing before work begins. Choose Gripped for pipeline, Extra Medium for leadership.

What Gripped does

Gripped is a B2B digital marketing agency based on Borough High Street in London, founded in 2017 by Steve Eveleigh and Ben Crouch. In its own words, it helps SaaS, AI and tech companies turn strategy and paid media into qualified pipeline that tracks straight to revenue, and it says it has done that for more than 160 companies.

The service list is a demand engine: go-to-market strategy, demand generation, account-based marketing, paid search, paid social, SEO, generative engine optimisation, content, martech automation and website design and development. Published clients include Ideagen, Epicor, Ravelin and Crownpeak. The team is around 25 people serving roughly 30 active clients, with an average client relationship of four years.

Where Extra Medium is different

Extra Medium is not a channel agency and not a sector specialist. You hire a leadership seat: a named, founder-led senior operator sits in your leadership meetings and owns positioning, the plan and the budget, rather than handing you a department of channel specialists. That model is set out on our fractional CMO in London page.

Behind that seat sits one studio executing brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR work, so strategy and delivery live under the same roof. The full picture is on our marketing agency in London page. Engagements are not limited to one sector, and the engagement shape and price are put in writing before any work begins.

Who should hire which

Hire Gripped if you are a B2B SaaS, AI or tech company, roughly Series A to growth stage with £2M to £50M in annual recurring revenue, and the problem is pipeline: you want a specialist demand team that lives in paid media, ABM and SEO for software buyers. That focus is deliberate. Gripped says it does not work with pre-seed companies, e-commerce or consumer brands.

Consider Extra Medium if the gap is leadership rather than a channel: no senior marketer owns the strategy, the board wants one accountable name, or your market sits outside SaaS entirely. If you need both, the honest sequence is leadership first, then channels pointed at a plan.

  • Gripped is a specialist demand agency for B2B SaaS; Extra Medium is a leadership seat with a studio behind it.
  • One hires channel execution on a retainer; the other puts a named senior operator in charge of the whole plan.
  • Extra Medium puts the engagement shape and price in writing before work begins.

Asked often.

Is Extra Medium a good alternative to Gripped?

It can be, if the thing you are missing is leadership rather than demand execution. Gripped is a specialist B2B SaaS agency; Extra Medium is a fractional CMO seat plus a studio, working across sectors. If your pipeline engine is the gap, Gripped is built for exactly that. If nobody senior owns your positioning, plan and budget, that is the gap Extra Medium fills.

Does Gripped publish its pricing?

Yes, and credit to them for it. Gripped publishes retainers of £5,000 to £15,000 a month on its own site, says most marketing leaders invest £8,000 to £12,000 a month, and works on rolling contracts with three months' notice. Extra Medium does not publish a rate card; instead, the engagement shape and price are put in writing before work begins, once the seat has been scoped to your organisation.

How does working with Extra Medium start?

It starts with a first conversation, free and specific to your situation. If the fractional CMO seat fits, you receive the engagement shape and price in writing before any work begins. If your need is smaller than the seat, we say so plainly. Engagements with UK companies run business to business, commonly structured outside IR35.
