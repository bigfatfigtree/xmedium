Straight answers

Need an SEO agency in Raleigh that treats the Triangle as one market?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Raleigh with SEO built into the studio, not outsourced. We do the technical fixes, the local search work and the content that makes Triangle companies the obvious answer when customers search — in Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill and the suburbs between them.

What SEO work actually is

Three jobs. Technical: a site that loads fast, structures its pages clearly and lets search engines and AI answer tools read what you actually do. Local: Google Business Profiles, reviews and location pages that win the searches with a city name in them, which is most of the searches that matter for local revenue. Content: pages that answer real questions better than anyone else in the market, written for the buyer and structured so search engines can quote them. It is unglamorous, compounding work, and it sits inside the same studio that runs your paid, social and web, so the SEO strategy and the rest of the marketing never drift apart.

One metro, three search markets

People in Cary search for Cary. People in Durham search for Durham. A single page titled for “the Triangle” rarely wins any of them, so local programs here are built city by city and suburb by suburb — Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill, Cary, Apex and the corridors between. Layer on the region’s other search economy: Research Triangle Park employers competing nationally for researchers and engineers who Google a company long before they apply. Your search presence is doing recruiting whether you planned it or not, and in a talent market this tight, that may be its highest-value job.

SEO without the snake oil

Nobody honest guarantees a number-one ranking, and anyone who does is selling you the guarantee instead of the work. What we commit to is the work itself — the technical fixes shipped, the pages built, the local profiles kept current — and reporting that connects search to calls, bookings and sales, not a screenshot of a ranking tool. Rankings are a means. The phone ringing is the metric. If that is the version of SEO you have been looking for, start with a note through the contact page.

Key takeaways

  • People in Cary search Cary and people in Durham search Durham. One Triangle page rarely wins all three.
  • SEO is not a trick played on Google. It is being the clearest answer when a customer asks.

Asked often.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can move things in weeks. Local search usually builds over a quarter. Content compounds over six to twelve months and then keeps paying. Anyone promising faster is describing paid ads or a shortcut with an expiry date.

Do we need separate pages for Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill?

Often, yes. Search behavior is local: people name their own city, and search engines reward pages that answer for that city specifically. One generic regional page is how companies end up invisible in all three.

How do we start?

In writing. Send your site and your market through the contact page (/contact/) and we will reply with what we would fix first and what the program would look like. No audit-by-ambush, no 40-page PDF of red ink.
