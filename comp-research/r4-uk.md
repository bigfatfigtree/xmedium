# Competitor research — UK markets (R4)

Researched 2026-11-15 for Extra Medium's "alternative / comparison" landing pages.
Every fact below comes from the agency's OWN website, read on 2026-11-15, with the source page URL given for each record. Nothing is taken from review sites, directories or third-party profiles.

Markets covered: London, Manchester.
Selection rule used: strongest local independent agencies a growing company or scale-up would genuinely compare. Global holding-company networks, directories and solo freelancers excluded.

## London

### Gripped

- **Name / website:** Gripped — https://gripped.io/
- **Positioning (their words):** "Digital Marketing Agency for SaaS and AI"; headline "Build Pipeline. Drive Growth. Scale With Confidence."; "Gripped is the B2B digital marketing agency that's helped 160+ SaaS, AI and tech companies turn strategy and paid media into qualified pipeline that tracks straight to revenue." (Source: https://gripped.io/)
- **Services they list:** GTM strategy, demand generation, account-based marketing (ABM), paid search, paid social, GEO (generative engine optimisation), SEO, website design & development. Their website page FAQ also lists content marketing, martech automation, lead generation and sales enablement. (Sources: https://gripped.io/ and https://gripped.io/b2b-website-design-agency/)
- **Size signals:** Founded 2017. "Approximately 25 staff" (homepage FAQ). Their About page says: "We're 25 people serving 30 active clients. Average client relationship is 4 years. 80% retention rate." Founders named on site: Steve Eveleigh and Ben Crouch, "have worked in B2B SaaS since 2004". Based at 140–148 Borough High Street, London SE1. (Sources: https://gripped.io/ and https://gripped.io/about-gripped-2/)
- **Published pricing:** Yes, on their own site. Retainers "£5K–£15K per month for strategic execution across core channels. Most marketing leaders invest £8K–£12K per month"; FAQ: "Retainers typically start at £5K/month." Rolling contracts with three months' notice. Website management "from £1,500 / month". (Sources: https://gripped.io/ and https://gripped.io/b2b-website-design-agency/)
- **Notable clients (published on their site):** Ideagen, Epicor, Ravelin and Crownpeak are named in their homepage FAQ; client logos on the homepage also include Espria, Searchlight Cyber, Octopus Money, Zapnito, Duetto and Redstor. (Source: https://gripped.io/)
- **Who they seem right for (their stated focus):** B2B SaaS, AI and tech companies only, Series A through growth stage, typically £2M–£50M ARR. They state they do not work with pre-seed / pre-product-market-fit companies, e-commerce, consumer brands or generalist B2B. (Source: https://gripped.io/)
- **How Extra Medium differs:** Gripped is a sector-exclusive execution agency — B2B SaaS only, channel teams on a £5K–£15K/mo retainer. Extra Medium puts a founder-led fractional CMO inside the business (senior leadership, not just channel execution) with a full studio under the same roof, works across sectors rather than SaaS only, and puts the engagement price in writing before work begins.

History in their own words: "In 2005, we started our digital life as Artemis Eight… Fast forward to 2010, we transformed into Found." Google Premier Partner. Based at The Johnson Building, 77 Hatton Garden, London EC1N. (Source: https://www.found.co.uk/about-us)

Extra Medium is founder-led and deliberately senior-small: a fractional CMO plus studio in a single engagement for companies that need marketing leadership, not a large specialist roster — with the price in writing before work begins.

## Manchester

### Dark Horse

- **Name / website:** Dark Horse — https://darkhorse.co/
- **Positioning (their words):** Homepage headline "PPC & SEO TO MAKE YOU SMILE AND THEM CRY"; "We help aspiring companies parade the bodies of their competition whilst swimming in money, through PPC, Paid Social and SEO… We take companies on a journey from 7 to 8 figures." Also: "The vigilante digital marketing agency that Manchester has been waiting for." They state they are a certified Google Premier Partner ("top 3% in the UK"). (Source: https://darkhorse.co/)
- **Services they list:** SEO (including technical SEO and digital PR), PPC, paid social. (Sources: https://darkhorse.co/ and https://darkhorse.co/seo-manchester/)
- **Size signals:** Their own current job descriptions (hosted on darkhorse.co) say "We are a team of 40 people" / "There are about 40 of us". Founder page on their site: "In 2019, John launched Dark Horse with the aim of creating one of the best search marketing agencies in the UK" (John Keating, founder & CEO). Based at Chapel House, 1 Borough Road, Altrincham, Greater Manchester. (Sources: https://darkhorse.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Job-Descriptions-SEO-Lead-09092026-4-1.pdf and https://darkhorse.co/team-member/john-keating/)
- **Published pricing:** Yes, on their own site: "Our PPC campaigns start at £1,250 per month" (PPC Manchester page FAQ). SEO campaigns "are charged on a monthly basis, and vary based on the scale of work you need" — no figure published. (Sources: https://darkhorse.co/ppc-manchester/ and https://darkhorse.co/seo-manchester/)
- **Notable clients (published on their site):** Case studies on their homepage: Rhino Safety, Avenue 85 and Maximuscle; their SEO page also features JADAK. (Sources: https://darkhorse.co/ and https://darkhorse.co/seo-manchester/)
- **Who they seem right for (their stated focus):** Companies that want aggressive, search-led growth — in their words, clients "range from small local businesses to international companies", with e-commerce and lead-generation a clear strength; the whole pitch is aimed at firms being outgunned by bigger competitors. (Sources: https://darkhorse.co/ and job descriptions on darkhorse.co)
- **How Extra Medium differs:** Dark Horse is a search-only specialist — SEO, PPC and paid social, no marketing leadership layer and no brand/creative studio. Extra Medium combines a founder-led fractional CMO (the senior leader who owns the whole plan) with a full studio under one roof, and puts the price in writing before work begins.
