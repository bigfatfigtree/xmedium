# CiteHawk Sources and Inclusion Pitches for Extra Medium

Researched 2026-11-16 from the frozen CiteHawk AI Index record, November 2026 edition,
"digital marketing agencies" (the 9 AI models' verbatim answers to "What are the best
digital marketing agencies?"), plus public signup and list pages checked the same day.

## How the counts work

CiteHawk counts 43 distinct source domains pooled across the 9 models for this category.
The frozen JSON exposes the sources inside the answer texts themselves: inline links,
footnotes, and sources a model names as its evidence. Some answers (Perplexity, Copilot)
carry numbered citations whose targets are not expanded in the JSON, so the identifiable
source set below is the actionable subset, counted conservatively.

"Cited in" below means: how many of the 9 verbatim answers link to the source or name it
as evidence. Link counts are given where one answer links a source repeatedly.

Headline finding: one directory dominates. Clutch is named or linked in 6 of the 9 answers,
and ChatGPT's entire shortlist is built from Clutch profile links. Everything else is a
long tail of one-answer sources, most of them listicles published by agencies themselves.

## 1. DIRECTORY — Extra Medium can create these profiles itself

Ranked by citations.

### 1. clutch.co — cited in 6 of 9 answers (start here)
- ChatGPT linked Clutch 6 times (5 to clutch.co profile and category pages, 1 to
  staging.clutch.co, the same directory on a staging host). Claude, DeepSeek, Grok,
  Google AI and Google AI Mode all name Clutch as the place to check reviews and rankings.
  Google AI Mode cites the "Clutch.co Digital Marketing Rankings" by name.
- Route: self-serve. Clutch offers a free Basic provider profile through its Get Listed
  flow (grow.clutch.co carries the Get Listed entry; help.clutch.co confirms providers
  create and manage their own profiles, one profile per company). Rankings inside Clutch
  run on verified client reviews, so the profile only starts working once reviews exist.
  A paid Verified tier (about $499 a year per Clutch's public provider pricing) is
  optional and not needed to be listed.
- Fit for Extra Medium: direct. Categories include digital marketing agencies by city,
  which lines up with the Virginia Beach, Hampton Roads, Richmond, Washington DC and
  other market pages already live on xmedium.co.

### 2. g2.com — cited in 3 of 9 answers
- Claude tells readers to check "Clutch and G2 profiles." DeepSeek lists G2 among the
  sources to use. Grok notes KlientBoost's "strong G2 feedback."
- Route: self-serve. G2 carries service provider categories (Digital Marketing, SEO
  Services, Content Marketing Agencies, PPC Services, Social Media Marketing Companies)
  and agency profiles show a "Claim this profile" path for the firm itself. Free listings
  are minimal and G2's paid vendor plans are priced for software companies, so the play
  here is claim the free profile and collect a few reviews, not a paid plan.
- Fit: secondary. Worth an hour, after Clutch.

### 3. semrush.com (Semrush Agency Directory / Agency Partners) — named in 4 of 9 answers, cited as an agency source in 1
- Google AI names the "Semrush Agency Directory" directly: agencies "listed on platforms
  like Clutch and Semrush Agency Directory." Gemini, Perplexity and DeepSeek name Semrush
  as a core tool, which is brand exposure rather than an agency citation.
- Route: self-serve with a cost. Per Semrush's own knowledge base, an agency joins via a
  Semrush account, My Profile, Agency Partners, "Add agency," and gets a personal Partner
  Page in the directory. Semrush's KB prices the Agency Partners platform at $90 a month.
  That fee is Jeff's call, flagged here rather than assumed.
- Fit: real but paid. The only directory on this list with a known monthly cost.

### 4. goodfirms.co — cited in 1 of 9 answers
- Grok links the GoodFirms digital marketing directory page directly
  (goodfirms.co/directory/marketing-services/top-digital-marketing-companies).
- Route: self-serve and free. GoodFirms runs a "Get Listed Free" entry (shown on
  goodfirms.co/about-us), with a free profile, review collection and directory placement,
  subject to an admin approval step before the profile goes live. Paid plans exist and
  are not needed to be listed.
- Fit: easy second tier. Free, an hour of work, modest payoff.

## 2. LISTICLE / ROUNDUP — pitchable

All of these are "best agencies" lists published by agencies or content sites. None of
them advertises a public submission form (checked 2026-11-16), so inclusion is an email
pitch to the publisher, which is what Pitch A below is for. Ranked by citations, then by
realism for a firm of Extra Medium's size.

### Cited in 1 of 9 answers, and named by Claude as self-published lists
Claude's answer opens by warning that Thrive, Silverback Strategies, Sweat Pants Agency
and Digital Success "each publish a list with themselves at or near the top." That warning
is itself the opportunity: these lists are how the models build their answers, and three
of the four publishers regularly include small and mid-size firms.

### 5. silverbackstrategies.com — Silverback Strategies (Washington DC)
- List: "Best Digital Marketing Agencies in the U.S. in 2026 (Ranked & Reviewed),"
  silverbackstrategies.com/lists/best-digital-marketing-agencies-in-the-us-rank-reviewed/,
  plus city and specialty editions, including a Washington DC list and a PPC list.
- Why it is realistic: the lists already include small firms (Verbsz Marketing of Reston,
  Virginia; Black Propeller of Bel Air, Maryland; Sagefrog of Doylestown, Pennsylvania).
  Silverback is DC based, and Washington DC is a market Extra Medium already works and
  publishes for. This is the strongest listicle target on the list.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email to the firm, referencing the
  specific list. Use Pitch A.

### 6. digitalsuccess.us — Digital Success (Dallas, Texas)
- Lists: self-first roundups including "GEO and AI Visibility Agencies in the U.S."
  (digitalsuccess.us/blog/us-companies-for-geo-and-ai-visibility-optimization.html) and
  Dallas and SaaS SEO lists.
- Why it is realistic: a mid-size Dallas firm writing about AI visibility, which is
  exactly Extra Medium's AI practice angle, and Dallas-Fort Worth is a market Extra
  Medium already publishes for. Their GEO list profiles only five agencies and explains
  its reasoning, so a short, factual pitch has a genuine opening.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email. Use Pitch A.

### 7. thriveagency.com — Thrive Internet Marketing Agency
- List: "20 Best Digital Marketing Agencies in 2026,"
  thriveagency.com/news/best-digital-marketing-agencies/. Thrive scores itself 98 of 100
  at number 1 and states the list is reviewed and updated monthly, weighted 60 percent
  toward AI search visibility.
- Why it is realistic: the monthly update cycle means new entries are actually added,
  and the list already carries mid-size firms. It is competitive, and a pitch should lead
  with the AI visibility angle their own methodology rewards.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email. Use Pitch A.

### 8. firstpagesage.com — First Page Sage
- List: "The Top Digital Marketing Agencies of 2026,"
  firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/top-digital-marketing-agencies/, plus a Top Marketing
  Agencies report and many vertical editions (an MSP and IT services GEO list, for
  example, includes small firms such as BigOrange Marketing and Tech Pro Marketing).
- Why it matters despite the odds: Copilot's whole answer is built on this publisher's
  analysis. Copilot repeats First Page Sage's own framing, ranks it first, and takes its
  number 2 (AMP Agency) from the same list. One inclusion here plausibly feeds a model
  answer directly.
- Realism: the flagship top 10 leans enterprise, so the better opening is a vertical or
  GEO edition where smaller firms already appear. Long shot, high value.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email. Use Pitch A.

### 9. hawksem.com — HawkSEM
- List: "Top PPC Agencies," hawksem.com/blog/top-ppc-agencies/, linked once in Grok's
  footnotes. HawkSEM also publishes best-agency roundups of its own.
- Realism: moderate. A PPC list is a narrower door, and paid media is one part of Extra
  Medium's offer rather than the whole of it. Worth one pitch, not a campaign.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email. Use Pitch A.

### 10. sweatpantsagency.com — Sweat Pants Agency
- List: "The Top 9 Digital Marketing Agencies in 2026,"
  sweatpantsagency.com/the-top-9-digital-marketing-agencies, with itself at number 1,
  plus DTC and CPG editions.
- Realism: low for Extra Medium. The list is built around DTC and ecommerce growth
  brands, which is not Extra Medium's center of gravity. Included for completeness
  because Claude names it; pitch only if a DTC angle is ever true.
- Route: no public submission form found. Pitch by email. Use Pitch A.

### A note on the ranked agencies themselves
The 25 ranked brands (WebFX, KlientBoost, SmartSites and the rest) are the subjects of
the answers, not sources, so they are not classified above. Two of them double as
sources and are already counted: Thrive (section 2) and HawkSEM (section 2). The tools
named inside answers (Semrush and Ahrefs as software, HubSpot, Google Ads and others)
are products people use, not agency sources; Semrush appears in section 1 only because
Google AI cites its agency directory.

### Pitch B decision
Not needed. Every directory in this record that carries agency listings (Clutch, G2,
Semrush Agency Partners, GoodFirms) offers a self-serve route to create or claim a
profile, so there is no directory here that requires a correction or inclusion letter.
The one partial exception is Gartner Peer Insights, classified not actionable above for
fit reasons rather than access reasons.

## 4. PITCH DRAFTS

Drafts only. Nothing sends without Jeff's approval. Written to the standing outreach
rules: humble, modest claims, no hype, no em dashes, no call ask, US spelling.

### Pitch A: to a listicle or roundup publisher

Subject: For your [list name] list, when you next update it

Hi [name or team],

Thank you for the work behind [list name] on [site]. I read it while researching how
buyers find agencies like ours, and it is one of the more careful lists of its kind.

I run Extra Medium, a marketing agency and fractional CMO firm based in Virginia Beach.
We are a senior-only studio, so the people in the meeting are the people who do the work.
Alongside client work we run an AI practice, and our Labs team builds platforms of our
own, including Magpie, a live platform that connects local businesses with local
creators, and Hapsta, which is live at hapsta.co. Before this I spent more than twelve
years running digital strategy for media companies, and I have worked on influencer
marketing at scale for large brands.

If it would help your readers to have a smaller firm in the mix, I would be grateful to
be considered the next time the list is updated. I am glad to send our service details,
the markets we work in, and anything else in writing that would make your job easier.

Thank you for your time, and for the list itself.

Jeff Rimmer
Founder, Extra Medium
xmedium.co

### Priority order when this gets used
1. Clutch profile first (not a pitch, a signup). It is the source the models lean on most.
2. Silverback Strategies list (DC firm, small firms already listed, a market we work).
3. Digital Success GEO and AI visibility list (our AI practice is the honest angle).
4. Thrive list (monthly updates, and their scoring rewards AI visibility).
5. First Page Sage vertical or GEO editions (long shot, feeds Copilot's answer).
6. GoodFirms and G2 free profiles, then Semrush only if Jeff approves the monthly fee.
