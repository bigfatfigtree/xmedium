# Competitor Research — North Carolina Markets (Raleigh / Charlotte / Greensboro / Wilmington)

Researched 2026-11-15 for Extra Medium "alternative / comparison" landing pages.
Method: local search results reviewed per market, then facts extracted from each agency's OWN website (read 2026-11-15). Every fact below carries its source page. No pricing, headcount, or client claims are taken from directories or review sites. Where a fact could not be confirmed on the agency's own site, it is marked "not published / not confirmed on own site."

Markets are kept separate: Raleigh (the whole Triangle — Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill as one market) and Charlotte are never merged.
