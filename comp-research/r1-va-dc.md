# Competitor Research — R1: Virginia + Washington DC

**Prepared for:** Extra Medium (xmedium.co) "alternative / comparison" landing pages
**Date read:** 2026-11-15
**Method:** Local search results reviewed per market, then facts extracted from each agency's OWN website (homepage, about, services, team, careers, case-study pages). Every fact below carries its source page. Where a fact could only be found in the company's own self-authored profile copy syndicated elsewhere (e.g., its own LinkedIn/Google business description or its own job postings), that is flagged explicitly. No numbers were taken from review sites or data brokers (ZoomInfo/RocketReach/LeadIQ figures were ignored).
