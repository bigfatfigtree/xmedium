# Competitor research — R3: Roanoke VA · Outer Banks NC · Chicago IL · Dallas TX

Researched 2026-11-15. Every fact below comes from the agency's OWN website, read that day; the source page URL is given with each record. No review-site numbers, no directory data, no invented figures. Where a site does not publish something (pricing, headcount, founding year), that is stated rather than filled in.

Extra Medium's contrast points (used only where factual): founder-led senior team; a fractional CMO seat plus a working studio under one roof; engagement shape and price in writing before work begins.

## Roanoke, VA

A real local scene — several independent firms with substantive sites. The two strongest general-market targets are Firefli (brand + digital) and LeadPoint Digital (inbound/digital marketing). Also present but niche, so not primary targets: AccessU (higher education specialist, accessu.com) and Anstey Hodge (senior living specialist, ansteyhodge.com).

- **Website:** https://firefli.com/
- **Positioning (their words):** "Bringing Digital Possibilities to Light" — "Because the strongest brands don't follow 'the expected', Firefli helps you challenge assumptions, uncover what matters most, and transform it into meaningful impact." Also: "Turning Bold Visions Into Meaningful Brands." (Source: https://firefli.com/)

(Source: https://firefli.com/features/)

- **How Extra Medium differs:** Firefli is a brand-and-web systems shop: the engagement centers on the digital property. Extra Medium leads with a senior fractional CMO sitting in the client's leadership seat, with the studio behind it — ongoing marketing leadership across every channel, not a build-and-steward model — and the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

## Outer Banks, NC

What exists is a pair of small, web-design-led local boutiques with real presence and long tenure — Outer Banks Media and Oasis Creative. Both are legitimate comparison targets for local buyers, but the comparison is against web shops, not against agencies of Extra Medium's shape. (Mitro Digital Marketing runs Outer Banks service pages but publishes a Raleigh address, so it is not an OBX-local firm and was excluded.)
