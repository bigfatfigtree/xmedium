Straight answers

Looking for an SEO agency in Austin?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Austin, and our SEO practice covers technical fixes, local search, content and AI search visibility. Austin buyers search before they buy anything, from brisket to B2B software. We make sure the companies worth finding are the ones that show up.

Search is Austin's front door

Every month, more people arrive in Austin knowing nobody. Their first move is a search: a dentist, a roofer, a lunch spot near South Congress, a software vendor for the new job. Locals search the same way, because the city changes too fast for habits to keep up. If your company does not show up for those searches, you are not in the running, full stop. As a marketing agency in Austin, we treat search as the front door and build everything behind it to deserve the click.

Technical, local and AI search

The work has three layers. Technical SEO keeps the site fast, crawlable and structured so search engines understand what you sell and where. Local SEO handles the map pack, listings and reviews that decide most Austin service searches. And AI search visibility is the newest layer: buyers increasingly ask AI tools for a shortlist, and those tools quote sources they can parse. We write and structure content so your company is one of the names they quote, and our Extra Extra newsroom is a working example of the approach.

SEO with an owner

Most SEO retainers fail quietly: a checklist gets worked, a report gets emailed, and nobody senior connects the effort to the business. Here, search sits inside one strategy owned by a senior operator. Priorities come from what will move revenue, content gets written to answer real Austin questions, and work that stops performing gets stopped. Search is a channel, not a department, and it is managed like one.

Asked often.

How long does SEO take to work?

Honest answer: months, not weeks. Technical fixes and local listings can move within the first quarter; competitive rankings and content authority compound over six to twelve months. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling you a story, not a service.

Do you handle local SEO for Austin businesses?

Yes. Local search is where most Austin service businesses win or lose: the map pack, your Google Business Profile, reviews, and pages built for the neighborhoods you actually serve, from downtown and South Congress to Round Rock and Cedar Park.

What is AI search visibility?

It is whether AI tools name and quote your company when buyers ask them for options. The inputs overlap with good SEO: clear structure, direct answers, consistent facts about your business across the web. We build for both at once.
