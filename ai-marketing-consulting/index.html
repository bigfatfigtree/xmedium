Straight answers

How can AI actually help a marketing team, beyond the demos?

AI helps a marketing team when it is pointed at a specific job with a measurable outcome: research that took a day taking an hour, first drafts that start from your own data instead of a blank page, agents that handle the repetitive middle of a workflow while people keep the judgment calls. Extra Medium’s Intelligence practice audits where AI actually pays inside your marketing operation, then builds the agents and automations — the same discipline we use on our own products, where an AI assistant works from each user’s own account data and nothing else.

Where AI pays first

The wins are boring and compounding: turning customer language into briefs, keeping content calendars fed with researched first drafts, summarizing campaign performance into decisions, qualifying and routing inquiries, and keeping a knowledge base the whole team can actually query. The pattern is the same everywhere — a clear job, a defined data boundary, a human approving anything that leaves the building.

What we build

Three shapes of work. AI audits: a map of your marketing workflows with the automation candidates ranked by payoff and risk. Agents: purpose-built assistants on your own data — like Maggie, the assistant inside Magpie, who answers only from the signed-in user’s account data, never from anyone else’s. Automation: the connective work between tools you already have, so the system runs while the team does the thinking.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell AI as a personality transplant for your brand, and we don’t ship anything that publishes, sends or spends without a human in the loop where it matters. AI that can’t show its work doesn’t go near a client’s customers. The standard is the one we run at home: useful, bounded, and honest about what it is.

Asked often.

Do we need our data cleaned up before starting with AI?

Some order helps, but a full data project is not a prerequisite. The audit starts from the workflows as they are and identifies the smallest data fixes that unlock the first wins.

Will AI replace our marketing team?

It replaces tasks, not judgment. Teams that adopt it well end up doing more of the work only people can do: strategy, taste, relationships, and the final call.

How is this different from buying an AI tool subscription?

Tools are generic; the value is in the fit to your workflows, your data boundaries and your approval rules. We build that fit, train the team on it, and leave the system documented and owned by you.
