Straight answers

Looking for a marketing agency in Charlotte that gets how this city does business?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Charlotte for companies that have outgrown referrals and luck. Banking and fintech, corporate headquarters, NASCAR and a decade of fast growth set the pace here. We bring the full mix — strategy, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer, creative and web — under one senior team.

Everything under one roof

Strategy, digital marketing, SEO, paid search and paid social, social media, influencer and creator marketing, content, creative and branding, web design, email and fractional CMO leadership — the full mix, in one studio, run by senior people. That structure matters more in Charlotte than most places. Companies here tend to arrive with a patchwork: a web firm from one era, an ads vendor from another, a PR relationship held together by habit. Each one reports success in its own language and the total still disappoints. One team writing one strategy and executing all of it is how the pieces start compounding instead of competing. The detail is on our services page.

Charlotte runs on relationships. Marketing has to earn them

This is a city where business still moves through trust: the bank towers Uptown, the headquarters economy, deals that start with who can vouch for you. Marketing that ignores that culture — loud, generic, imported from a national template — bounces off. Marketing that understands it works like a good introduction: it proves credibility before the first meeting, keeps your name in the right rooms from South End to Ballantyne, and gives the referral you were hoping for something substantial to land on. And because Charlotte sprawls, it also has to work at neighborhood distance, for the customer in Huntersville or Fort Mill who will never drive Uptown for anything.

Senior people, straight answers

Engagements start with diagnosis, not a package: what is actually limiting growth, what we would do first, what it costs, in writing. Companies that need direction more than deliverables can begin with the fractional CMO seat and add the studio as the team underneath it. Companies that know exactly what they need can go straight to the work. Either way the first step is a note through the contact page, answered by a person who will be in the room for the work itself.

Key takeaways

  • Charlotte business runs on relationships. Marketing’s job is to make the right ones start sooner.
  • A referral got you here. It will not get you to the next stage by itself.
  • One senior team across strategy, search, paid, social and creative beats a vendor for every channel.

Asked often.

Do you only work with banks and large companies?

No. Finance is Charlotte’s center of gravity, not its whole economy. We work with growth-stage companies, professional services, healthcare, consumer brands and local businesses — the common thread is a leadership team that wants senior marketing without building a department first.

Most of our business comes from referrals. Why change anything?

Referrals are a reputation with no distribution. They cap out at the size of your network and arrive on their own schedule. Marketing does not replace them; it makes the reputation findable by everyone who is not already in the room, and gives referred prospects something convincing to find.

How do we start?

In writing. Send what you are working on through the contact page (/contact/) and we will reply with whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost.
