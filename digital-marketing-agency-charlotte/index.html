Straight answers

What should a digital marketing agency in Charlotte do differently in a banking town?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Charlotte where digital means the whole system: SEO, paid search, paid social, email, content and a website built to convert. Charlotte buyers research quietly and buy on trust, so we build digital that proves credibility first and asks for the meeting second.

The system, not the channel of the month

SEO, paid search, paid social, email, content and web work as one system or they work against each other: ads sending traffic to pages that say something different, content written for search engines instead of the CFO reading it, a beautiful website that never asks for anything. We sequence it from one strategy — search presence that compounds, paid media that buys speed only where speed pays, email that works the customers you already have, and a site engineered to turn research into conversations. Every element is listed on our services page; the discipline is in running them as one program with one owner.

Built for Charlotte’s sprawl and sectors

Charlotte is not one audience. It is a finance and fintech core where buyers are compliance-minded and allergic to hype, major healthcare and energy employers, a NASCAR-and-banking mix that confuses every national template, and a sprawl of distinct sub-markets — Ballantyne, South End, NoDa, Lake Norman, Matthews, and Fort Mill just over the state line. Digital programs here have to segment by neighborhood and industry at the same time, and they double as recruiting: in a city importing talent this fast, your search presence is being read by future employees as carefully as by future customers.

Reported against revenue

Reports lead with what came in: leads, pipeline, sales, and what each channel contributed, with the failures named in the same document as the wins. When a channel underperforms, it gets fixed or it gets cut, and the budget moves to what is working. That standard is easier to keep when the team reporting is the team that did the work. Start with a note through the contact page describing what you sell and where the current program feels stuck.

Key takeaways

  • In Charlotte, your website is the first reference check. Buyers decide before they ever call.
  • Digital works when the channels share one strategy and one number: revenue.
  • Sprawl is a targeting problem. Ballantyne and Lake Norman are different markets on the same map.

Asked often.

We are in a regulated industry. Can digital marketing still work?

Yes, with the volume set correctly. In finance, fintech and healthcare the winning digital is credibility content, search presence and proof — the material a careful buyer needs to shortlist you. It avoids hype because your buyers, and your compliance team, both punish it.

How fast will we see results?

Paid search and paid social can produce pipeline within weeks. SEO and content compound over quarters. A sane program funds both clocks: speed where it is worth buying, and a base that lowers the cost of every future customer.

How do we start?

In writing. Send the details through the contact page (/contact/) — what you sell, who buys it, what you have tried — and we will reply with what we would do first and what it would cost.
