Where Extra Medium is different

Sage Island sells execution: a full-service team that does the work. Extra Medium sells leadership first. As a marketing agency in Wilmington, Extra Medium starts with a fractional CMO seat: a named senior operator in your leadership meetings who owns positioning, plan and budget, with the studio behind the seat to execute brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR.

The trade-offs are the traditional model's, not any one firm's. An execution shop works the plan you bring: departments handle their pieces, account relationships manage the flow, and nobody inside the arrangement is accountable for whether the strategy itself is right. Strategy in one place, execution in another is how marketing ends up busy and ownerless. And with no published pricing, the number arrives after the courtship. Extra Medium starts at the other end: the senior operator who sets the plan sits in your meetings, the studio in the same house executes it, and the price is in writing before work begins.

So a fractional CMO in Wilmington from Extra Medium is the person accountable for whether the marketing works, not only whether it ships. The engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.
