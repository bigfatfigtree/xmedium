Straight answers

Asked, answered.

What does Extra Medium do?

Extra Medium is a strategy, creative, growth and technology studio based in Virginia Beach, Virginia. We launch new businesses and products, reinvent brands that have outgrown their look, and run growth systems — paid media, search, creators and AI — measured against revenue, not vanity metrics.

What is Extra Medium Labs?

Extra Medium Labs is where the studio builds and operates its own products instead of only advising clients. Its flagship is SIGNL, a local creator marketing platform live in 37 founding markets across 8 countries, where local businesses comp or pay local creators to visit and post honestly, with every visit tracked.

Who does Extra Medium work with?

Founder-led and high-growth companies, pre-launch startups, manufacturers, consumer brands and established enterprises up to Fortune 100 scale — across consumer products, ecommerce, healthcare, retail, media, music, hospitality, professional services and SaaS. The common thread is ambition, not industry.

How does an engagement start?

With a conversation, not a proposal. Engagements take three shapes: fractional CMO leadership, a defined project such as a launch or rebrand, or ongoing growth work. If we are not the right fit, we say so — and usually know who is.
