Straight answers

What does social media marketing in Chicago look like when locals run it?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Chicago, and social is where the city talks back. We plan content around how Chicago actually lives, its neighborhoods, teams and food arguments, and we put local creators in the work through Magpie, our own local creator platform. Less broadcasting, more belonging.

Social that sounds like the city

Chicago can smell a generic post from a mile off. The city runs on specifics: which side of the Sox-Cubs line you stand on, the correct order of an Italian beef, neighborhoods with their own accents and loyalties, a summer festival season that takes over the calendar. Social content that works here is built from that material, planned around the city’s real year — the river turning green, the first warm day everyone loses their mind, the long winter that moves life indoors and online. Stock content with a skyline photo pasted on is not a strategy; it’s a tell.

Creators over commercials

The most trusted voices in any Chicago neighborhood are the people who live there and post like it. That’s why we built Magpie, our local creator platform, in Extra Medium Labs: it connects businesses with local creators whose audiences are actually local, and shows each creator’s local reach before anyone books. For Chicago brands that means creator and influencer work built on real neighborhood audiences instead of follower counts inflated by people three time zones away.

A calendar, not a content pile

Social fails when it’s a pile of posts and works when it’s a calendar: the city’s moments mapped in advance, a steady rhythm of original content between them, and paid support behind the pieces that earn it. We run social as part of the wider growth system, so the audience built on social is the same audience search, email and creators are working on. One audience, one strategy, several doors in.

Key takeaways

  • Chicago can smell a generic post; specifics are the whole strategy.
  • A local creator with a real neighborhood audience beats a big account three time zones away.
  • Social works as a calendar built on the city’s year, not a pile of posts.

Asked often.

Do you work with Chicago influencers and creators?

Yes, and we built the platform for it. Magpie, from our own lab, matches businesses with local creators and shows each creator’s local reach up front, so a neighborhood restaurant or retailer can book people whose followers are genuinely nearby.

Which platforms matter most for Chicago businesses?

The ones your customers already use, which for most local brands means Instagram and TikTok first, with LinkedIn carrying the B2B side. We’d rather run two platforms with a real point of view than five with a posting schedule and no pulse.

How do we start?

In writing, through the contact page. Tell us the brand and the neighborhoods that matter. We’ll come back with what we’d post, who we’d put in it, and what we’d stop doing.
