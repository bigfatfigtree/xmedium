Looking for an alternative to French/West/Vaughan in the Triangle?

French/West/Vaughan is a traditional agency in the Triangle — 100+ experts, five offices, departments and account layers. Extra Medium is the other model: a fractional CMO in your leadership meetings, a senior-only studio behind the seat, AI and platform builders in-house, and the price in writing before work begins. One hires scale; the other hires leadership.

What French/West/Vaughan does

French/West/Vaughan (FWV) is a Raleigh-based public relations, public affairs, advertising and digital media agency founded in 1997. It employs more than 100 experts across offices in Raleigh, New York City, Los Angeles, Detroit and Tampa, and runs specialist divisions for pet and veterinary marketing (FWV Fetching), film production (Prix Productions) and New York PR (AMP3). Published work includes Mitsubishi Electric, the Wrangler x Barbie collaboration launch, Pendleton Whisky and the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians. FWV is a member of IPREX, a network of 75 independent PR and marketing agencies. It does not publish pricing.

Where Extra Medium is different

The difference is the model. With FWV you hire a department agency: a large team, account layers and specialist divisions. With Extra Medium you hire a leadership seat. As a marketing agency in Raleigh and across the Triangle, Extra Medium puts a named senior operator in your leadership meetings who owns your positioning, plan and budget, and the studio behind that seat executes the work: brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR.

That structure has a cost side, and it is the standard cost of the traditional agency model. Departments mean layers between you and the people doing the work: the senior names pitch the account, account teams run it day to day, and your message gets translated at every handoff. Strategy sits in one silo, execution in another, and no single person owns the whole result. And because the model does not publish pricing, the number only arrives after the courtship. Extra Medium is built the other way on each point: one senior operator in your meetings who owns the plan, the same house executing it, and the price in writing before anyone starts.

Extra Medium treats the whole Triangle, Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill, as one market, the way your customers actually move around it. And the commercial terms are plain: if a fractional CMO in Raleigh is the right fit, the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins. No scope fog, no surprise retainer math.
