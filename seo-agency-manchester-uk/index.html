Straight answers

Choosing an SEO agency in Manchester: what actually matters?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Manchester, and SEO is one of the practices under its roof. We build search the way you build anything that lasts: sound technical foundations, pages that answer the questions your customers actually ask, and local authority across Greater Manchester, reported in rankings and revenue.

Search across ten boroughs

Greater Manchester is ten boroughs with ten different characters: Salford and MediaCityUK, Stockport’s independents, Trafford, Bury, Rochdale, Oldham, Tameside, Wigan, Bolton. Customers search locally and specifically — a service, a town, a problem — and they also search nationally, where your competitors include every London agency’s client with a Manchester landing page and a bigger budget. Winning here means being genuinely the best answer at both levels: properly local pages for the borough searches, and substance strong enough to hold its own in the national ones.

What we actually do

Three layers, in order. Technical first: crawlability, speed and structure, the unglamorous repairs that decide whether anything else counts. Then content: pages that answer the questions your buyers genuinely ask, in the order they ask them — our straight answers pages are the working example of the approach. Then authority: links and mentions earned from the publications, partners and communities your market trusts. Splitting those layers between vendors is how each blames the others; they are one job on our services page.

Built for Google and the AI answers

Search now has two front doors: the results page, and the AI answer sitting above it. Both reward the same thing — a direct, honest answer to a real question, stated early and supported properly. Pages written that way get ranked, and they get quoted by the answer engines your customers increasingly ask first. Manchester businesses do not need more content. They need pages that say the true thing plainly and early, on a site built well enough to be believed.

Key takeaways

  • Greater Manchester searches by borough and by problem. Be the best answer at both levels or be neither.
  • Technical, content and authority are one job. Split them and each vendor blames the others.
  • Write the direct answer first: it wins the results page and gets quoted by the AI answer above it.

Asked often.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can move things in weeks. Competitive rankings take months of consistent work, because you are displacing businesses that have been building for years. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling the promise, not the result.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and you should walk away from anyone who does. Rankings depend on competitors and on Google, neither of which an agency controls. What we guarantee is the work: audits, pages, authority building, and reporting in rankings and revenue that you can interrogate.

How do we start?

In writing. Send us the site and the towns and terms you want to win through our contact page. We will tell you plainly where you stand and what we would do first.
