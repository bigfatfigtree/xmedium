Looking for an alternative to Bouvier Kelly in Greensboro?

Bouvier Kelly is a Greensboro full-service agency founded in 1974, its 15-person team covering branding, media, advertising, web, PR and digital. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Greensboro with a leaner model: a fractional CMO seat plus a studio under one roof, founder-led and senior-only, price in writing before work begins. Full-service traditional: Bouvier Kelly. Leadership first: Extra Medium.

What Bouvier Kelly does

Bouvier Kelly has been Greensboro's full-service agency since 1974, when founder Lou Kelly turned down a move to a larger market to start his own agency at home. Under the tagline 'Marketing That Makes a Difference,' it describes itself as one of North Carolina's premier full-service marketing agencies, with a goal-driven, ROI-focused approach and a team page that names 15 people, including its President & CEO, Executive Vice President and VP & Creative Director.

The service list is the classic full-service bench: branding, competitive analysis, buyer personas and research, media planning, print and digital advertising, website design and development, out-of-home and radio, email campaigns, SEO/SEM, video production, trade shows, social media, brand management, public relations, crisis management and influencer marketing. Five decades in one market is its own credential.

Where Extra Medium is different

The difference is who you actually work with. A traditional full-service agency runs on account management: you brief an account team, and they carry your requests to the specialists. Extra Medium has no account layer. As a marketing agency in Greensboro, it is founder-led and senior-only: the fractional CMO who sets your positioning, plan and budget is in your meetings, and the studio in the same house executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR.

That makes a fractional CMO in Greensboro from Extra Medium a leadership hire, not a vendor hire. And the terms are plain from the start: the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

Who should hire which

Hire Bouvier Kelly if you want a traditional full-service agency with deep Triad roots: one established firm to handle branding, media, PR and digital through an account team, with 50 years of local relationships behind it. For plenty of Greensboro companies, that is exactly the right shape.

Hire Extra Medium if the missing piece is not capacity but command: marketing is happening in pieces, no senior person owns the whole plan, and you want the leader and the studio in one engagement. Different structure, different job.
