What Bouvier Kelly does

Bouvier Kelly is a full-service marketing agency in Greensboro, founded in 1974. Its team page names 15 people, including its President & CEO, Executive Vice President and VP & Creative Director. The published service list covers branding, competitive analysis, buyer personas and research, media planning, print and digital advertising, website design and development, out-of-home and radio, email campaigns, SEO/SEM, video production, trade shows, social media, brand management, public relations, crisis management and influencer marketing. It does not publish pricing.

Where Extra Medium is different

The difference is who you actually work with. A traditional full-service agency runs on account management: you brief an account team, and they carry your requests to the specialists. Extra Medium has no account layer. As a marketing agency in Greensboro, it is founder-led and senior-only: the fractional CMO who sets your positioning, plan and budget is in your meetings, and the studio in the same house executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR.

Those are the standard trade-offs of the traditional model, and they compound. The senior people pitch; the account team runs the account; your brief gets translated at every handoff between departments. Strategy is set in one silo and executed in another, so accountability for the result belongs to no one in particular. And the pricing follows the same pattern: nothing published, so the number arrives after the courtship. Extra Medium is the opposite on each point: no account layer, the plan's owner in your meetings, one house for strategy and execution, and the price in writing before work begins.

That makes a fractional CMO in Greensboro from Extra Medium a leadership hire, not a vendor hire. And the terms are plain from the start: the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

The part most agencies cannot do

Then there is the building. Extra Medium runs a lab that ships products, not just campaigns: a live creator marketplace (Magpie) and a live platform for ticketed celebrity sessions (Hapsta) were both designed, built and launched by this team. If growth needs a platform under it, a product, an app, a tool your customers log into, you do not have to hire a second firm and teach it your business.

Its Intelligence practice does the same on the AI side: getting companies cited by AI search, building research and content systems, and deploying AI assistants with a hard privacy rule, a company’s data answers only its own questions. The operator in the seat has run digital strategy for media companies and influencer marketing at scale for large brands, so the advice comes from having shipped it.
