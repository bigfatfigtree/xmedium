Straight answers

How do you choose an SEO agency in Charlottesville?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Charlottesville, and our SEO practice is built for how this city actually gets searched: by locals choosing a regular spot, students new in town, alumni planning a trip back, and visitors deciding where to eat, stay and taste wine this weekend.

Four searchers, four intents

The same business gets searched four different ways here. A local types “near me” and trusts reviews. A first-year student searches like a newcomer, because she is one. An alumnus searches from three states away, planning around a football weekend. A visitor searches weeks out, comparing wineries, inns and restaurants she has never heard of. SEO that treats these as one audience writes one page and ranks for none of them. We build for each intent deliberately — different questions, different pages, different proof.

The local foundation

Most Charlottesville searches end at the map and the review profile, so the foundation is unglamorous and decisive: an accurate, complete Google Business Profile, categories set correctly, photos that look like the place, and a steady, honest review habit. On the site itself: clean technical basics, pages for the services and experiences people actually search for, and local detail a generic page cannot fake — neighborhoods, the Downtown Mall, the wine trail, the university calendar. These foundations sit inside our wider services practice.

Content that answers the trip

Visitors plan with questions: where to taste wine near Monticello, where to stay for graduation, what to do on a rainy weekend in Charlottesville. The businesses that answer those questions well — in guides, itineraries and genuinely useful pages — get found at the planning stage, when the whole trip is still winnable. It is the same discipline behind our own Straight Answers pages: answer the real question, in plain words, and let search and AI tools quote you for it.

Key takeaways

  • In Charlottesville, ‘near me’ has four different meanings. Rank for all of them.
  • Reviews are the loudest local signal you do not write yourself. Earn them on purpose.
  • Visitors search before they arrive. Be findable from three states away.

Asked often.

How important are reviews for Charlottesville SEO?

Very. In a small market, reviews are both a ranking signal and the tiebreaker readers actually use — a visitor choosing between two tasting rooms reads the recent reviews, not the websites. The work is earning them with a good experience and asking at the right moment, then answering them like a business that reads its own mail.

Can SEO bring us visitors, or only locals?

Both, but on different clocks. Local intent converts this week; visitor intent has to be won weeks earlier, from wherever the visitor lives, with planning content. A Charlottesville SEO plan that ignores the planning stage hands the highest-value searches to whoever wrote the best guide — often not a local business at all.

How do we start?

In writing. Send the problem through our contact page and you will get a specific reply: whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost. Nobody books a call on your calendar unless you ask for one.
