Straight answers

What does a fractional CMO do for an Outer Banks business?

A fractional CMO gives an Outer Banks company senior marketing leadership part-time, without a full-time executive hire. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in the Outer Banks, and its fractional CMO practice puts a named senior operator in charge of your plan, your calendar and your spend, with the studio behind the seat.

Who needs a CMO on the islands

The gap usually appears quietly. A vacation rental company grows from forty homes to two hundred. A restaurant group opens its third location. A wedding venue or charter operation becomes the default name in its category. Marketing grew along the way by accretion: a website company here, a social freelancer there, listing platforms taking their cut, and the owner still making every real decision at midnight in the middle of peak season, which is exactly when there is no time to make them well. Nobody senior owns the whole picture. That is the seat a fractional CMO fills, sized to a business that needs the judgment without a year-round executive salary. Our fractional CMO page explains the model in full.

What the seat does across the OBX calendar

On the Outer Banks, the calendar is the strategy. The fractional CMO runs the year as a sequence: the winter booking season, when rates, offers and campaigns decide the summer; the spring shoulder, when events, fishing and weddings need filling; peak season, when the job is delivery and capturing the content and reviews the next year will sell with; and the fall rebuild, when the website, brand and content get fixed while there is time. Vendors stop working in separate directions because one person owns the plan they serve, and leadership gets reporting against bookings and revenue, not activity.

How it works, and what it costs

Engagements run on a monthly cadence, typically one to three days a week of senior time, weighted toward the months when decisions get made. For context, published US market ranges for fractional CMO retainers typically run $3,000–$20,000 a month depending on cadence and scope; that is a market range, not our price. Extra Medium puts the engagement shape and the exact price in writing before any work begins, and the seat comes with the studio behind it, so the plan does not wait on hiring. The cost breakdown page goes deeper on how the pricing works.

Key takeaways

  • On the Outer Banks, the marketing calendar is the strategy: booking season decides the year.
  • A fractional CMO owns the whole plan, so your website company, freelancer and platforms stop working in separate directions.
  • Seasonal businesses need senior leadership most in the quiet months, when next season is actually won.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

Published US market ranges for fractional CMO retainers typically run $3,000 to $20,000 a month, set by days per week and scope. We never quote before understanding the business. You get our engagement shape and price in writing before work begins, with no surprises later.

Is a fractional CMO worth it for a seasonal business?

Seasonality is the argument for it. A full-time executive is a fixed year-round cost. A fractional seat can weight its time toward the booking season, when marketing decisions are actually made, and lighten up when the islands are full and the job is delivery.
