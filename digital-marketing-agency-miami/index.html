Straight answers

What should a digital marketing agency in Miami actually run for you?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Miami that runs digital as one system. SEO, paid search, paid social, email, web and analytics are planned together and judged against revenue, whether the customer is a visitor booking from Chicago or a local in Brickell. One team, one dashboard, no telephone game.

One system, not six subscriptions

The usual Miami setup is a paid media buyer in one tool, an SEO retainer in another, a website nobody has touched since the last redesign, and four reports that each claim victory while revenue stays flat. We run the digital stack as one system: search, paid, social, email and web planned against the same strategy and the same revenue line. When one channel moves, the same team adjusts the others, which is where the compounding comes from. One dashboard, agreed numbers, no telephone game between vendors.

Two audiences, one dashboard

Digital behavior in Miami splits cleanly in two. Visitors plan from somewhere else: they search weeks out, compare hotels, restaurants and experiences, and book around flights, cruises and event dates. Locals search by neighborhood: Wynwood for dinner, Brickell for lunch, Coral Gables, Doral and Aventura for everything else. A digital program built for this city structures campaigns for both, with visitor campaigns timed to booking windows and local campaigns built neighborhood by neighborhood. Average the two together and you reach neither.

AI search and the events calendar

Two forces now shape Miami digital. The first is the calendar: Art Basel week, the boat show, Ultra and the winter season spike demand on predictable dates, so budgets are planned around those spikes instead of reacting to them. The second is AI search: visitors increasingly ask AI tools where to stay, eat and go, and those tools quote sources that answer plainly. Our AI practice works on exactly that: AI search visibility, research and content systems, and AI assistants that answer only from a client’s own data. Being the answer, in both search engines and AI tools, is now part of the digital job.

Key takeaways

  • Miami digital has two front doors: visitors planning trips and locals choosing where to spend.
  • Event weeks spike demand on known dates; budgets planned around the calendar beat budgets that react.
  • The most valuable line in a report is what got stopped, and why.

Asked often.

Which digital channels should a Miami company start with?

The ones closest to existing demand. For most Miami companies that is search first, organic and paid, because it captures visitors planning trips and locals ready to buy. Social, creators and email then build the audience search has not reached yet.

Do you plan campaigns around Miami's events calendar?

Yes. Art Basel, the boat show, festival weekends and the winter season move demand in this city on dates everyone can see a year out. Budgets and creative are planned against that calendar, so the spike is captured instead of chased.

How do we start?

In writing, through the contact page. Bring the site, the spend, or just the suspicion that the numbers do not add up. We will tell you what we would examine first and what we would stop paying for.
