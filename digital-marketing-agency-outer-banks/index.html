Straight answers

What does a digital marketing agency do for an Outer Banks business?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in the Outer Banks, and digital is where the season gets booked. Guests plan months ahead, mostly on their phones. We run paid search, paid social, email and your booking path so they find you, choose you, and book direct instead of through a platform.

Your guests plan on their phones, months ahead

The Outer Banks is a drive-to destination, and the drive starts on a screen. Families in Richmond, Hampton Roads, Raleigh, DC and Ohio plan in the winter, research on their phones, compare houses and restaurants across a dozen open tabs, and book long before they cross the Wright Memorial Bridge. The path runs search, reviews, your website, booking. If any step leaks, a listing platform happily collects the booking and keeps the commission, plus the guest relationship. Digital marketing here is mostly the discipline of making that path yours, end to end.

The channels, and the job each one does

Paid search captures booking intent: the traveler searching for a pet-friendly house in Duck or a fishing charter in Hatteras is ready to spend, and the results page decides who gets the call. Paid social builds demand earlier, putting the islands in front of drive-distance audiences while they are still choosing where to go, and it is the workhorse for shoulder-season offers. Email to past guests is the highest-value channel on the islands, because a family that stayed with you once is the cheapest booking you will ever win. Retargeting and a mobile-first booking path close the loop. Our services page covers the full digital mix.

Direct bookings are the whole point

Keep the platforms; they are good shop windows. But every booking that comes direct skips the platform’s cut and gives you the guest’s email address, which is how next year’s booking gets cheaper. We report like owners: spend against bookings and revenue, channel by channel, in plain language. What does not produce gets stopped, and the budget moves to what does. That discipline matters most on the Outer Banks, where the difference between a good and a great season is usually decided before the first guest arrives.

Key takeaways

  • Every direct booking is one you did not pay a platform to deliver, and a guest you can bring back.
  • Your past-guest email list is the most valuable marketing asset on the Outer Banks.
  • Outer Banks guests plan months ahead on their phones. A booking path not built for that is built for your competitors.

Asked often.

Can digital marketing really fill the shoulder seasons?

It is the main lever that does. Shoulder-season demand exists: fall fishing, spring breaks, remote-work stays, weddings and events. Those guests plan differently and book later than summer families, and paid social, past-guest email and drive-market offers are how you reach them while your competitors wait for summer.

We get most bookings through Vrbo and Airbnb. Why change?

Do not drop them; they put your property in front of millions. But every direct booking skips the platform cut and gives you the guest’s email, which compounds year after year. Most healthy Outer Banks businesses run both, with the direct share growing each season.

How do we start?

In writing. Send a note through our contact page with what you run and where your bookings come from now. You will get a specific reply on what we would fix first and what it would cost.
