Looking for an alternative to Wray Ward in Charlotte?

Wray Ward is a traditional agency in Charlotte — a single-category specialist for home and building brands, run through departments and account teams. What Wray Ward does

Wray Ward is a Charlotte agency specializing in one category: home and building brands. It describes itself as the largest agency in the country specializing in the category. Its published capabilities cover brand building, connections, creative and trade solutions, including strategy and planning, integrated media, search marketing and e-commerce, performance and analytics, and public relations, including media relations, corporate communications, media training and influencer marketing. Featured work on its site includes Pinehurst, Mastic, VELUX and Huber Engineered Woods. It does not publish pricing.

Where Extra Medium is different

Extra Medium is not a category specialist. As a marketing agency in Charlotte, it works across categories, and the thing it sells first is leadership: a fractional CMO seat. A named senior operator sits in your leadership meetings and owns positioning, plan and budget, and the studio behind the seat executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR.

The traditional agency model carries the same trade-offs here as everywhere. Departments put layers between you and the work: senior people pitch, account teams run the account, and strategy and execution sit in separate silos, so the plan you approved is rarely the plan the specialists are working from. And with no published pricing, the number arrives after the courtship, quoted privately once the relationship is underway. Extra Medium flips each point: the person who sets the plan sits in your meetings and answers for it, the studio executing it is in the same house, and the price is in writing before work begins.

With a department agency you brief an account team and the work moves through the building. With a fractional CMO in Charlotte from Extra Medium, the person who sets the plan is the person accountable for it, and the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.
