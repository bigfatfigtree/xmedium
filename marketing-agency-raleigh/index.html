Straight answers

Looking for a marketing agency in Raleigh that treats the Triangle as one market?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Raleigh serving the whole Triangle — Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill as one market. We run strategy, digital, SEO, paid media, social, influencer marketing, content and web under one roof, led by senior people. One team, one plan, no hand-offs between five vendors.

Everything under one roof

Most companies here don’t have a marketing problem. They have a coordination problem: one vendor on SEO, another on ads, a freelancer on social, and nobody owning the whole picture. Extra Medium is built the other way. Strategy, digital marketing, SEO, paid search and paid social, social media, influencer and creator marketing, content, creative and branding, web design, email and fractional CMO leadership all sit in one studio, so the plan is written once and executed by the people who wrote it. The full list lives on our services page; the short version is that if it shapes how customers find you, judge you or buy from you, it belongs in the same room.

Built for how the Triangle actually works

Raleigh is not one market with three names. It is three distinct cities wired together by I-40 and a shared workforce: research and biotech in Research Triangle Park, Duke and healthcare in Durham, UNC and a college-town economy in Chapel Hill, NC State and state government in Raleigh, and fast-growing suburbs like Cary and Apex filling in between. Your customers, your hires and your competitors all move across that map daily. Marketing here has to speak to engineers and researchers without boring everyone else, recruit in one of the tightest talent markets in the South, and still work for the restaurant group or home-services company whose customers never leave a ten-mile radius. That range is the job, and it is why a generalist national playbook underperforms here.

Senior people, one conversation to start

The practice is senior-only and founder-led: diagnosis first, one written strategy everyone argues from, then the calendar, the team and the vendors run against it. Companies that need leadership more than hands can start with the fractional CMO seat and use the studio as the team underneath it. Either way, it starts in writing — send the details through our contact page and you will get a specific reply, not a sales sequence.

Key takeaways

  • Raleigh is three cities sharing one workforce, and marketing here has to work in all of them.
  • One senior team for strategy, search, paid, social and creative beats five vendors passing a brief around.
  • The Triangle buys on evidence. Marketing here should be as rigorous as the research it sits next to.

Asked often.

Do you work across the whole Triangle, or just Raleigh?

The whole Triangle: Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill are one market to us, plus the suburbs between them like Cary, Apex and Morrisville. Most customers and employees cross those lines every day, so the marketing should too.

What kinds of Triangle companies do you work with?

The mix this region actually has: technology and biotech around the Park, healthcare, professional services, state-government-adjacent firms, universities and the businesses that serve them, and local consumer brands. Startups through large companies — the common thread is a leadership team that wants senior marketing without building a department first.

How do we start?

In writing. Send what you are working on through the contact page (/contact/) and we will reply with whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost. No pressure, and no meeting you have to sit through to get a straight answer.
