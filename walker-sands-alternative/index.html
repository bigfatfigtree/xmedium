Looking for an alternative to Walker Sands in Chicago?

Founded in 2001, Walker Sands is a B2B marketing and PR agency with 100+ clients across Chicago, Seattle and Boston offices. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Chicago: a fractional CMO seat plus a studio under one roof, founder-led, senior-only, price in writing first. Walker Sands fits enterprise B2B programs; Extra Medium fits companies needing a chief.

What Walker Sands does

Walker Sands describes itself as a full-service B2B integrated marketing and public relations agency, and its method is branded Outcome-based Marketing. Its services run from public relations, demand generation, branding, creative services and marketing strategy through paid digital, SEO, GEO, conversion rate optimization, marketing automation, account-based marketing, content marketing, analytics and revenue operations.

Founded in 2001, the firm says it serves 100+ clients from offices in Chicago, Seattle and Boston, and it focuses exclusively on B2B, with practices in enterprise software and SaaS, fintech, healthcare, manufacturing, supply chain and logistics, and edtech. Client logos published on its about page include Sophos, Echo, Oak Street Health and Ensono.

Where Extra Medium is different

Walker Sands is built to run integrated B2B programs at scale, with a deep roster of specialists. Extra Medium is a smaller, different shape: you hire a leadership seat, not a department agency. A named senior operator sits in your leadership meetings and owns positioning, plan and budget, and the studio behind that seat executes brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid, social, influencer and PR. The model is laid out on our Chicago marketing agency page and the fractional CMO in Chicago page.

It is founder-led and senior-only: the person who sets the strategy is the person in the room. And the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

Who should hire which

Hire Walker Sands when you are a B2B company, mid-market or enterprise, that needs an integrated PR-plus-demand program run at scale: a SaaS, fintech, healthcare or manufacturing firm that wants one established firm coordinating many specialists across offices.

Hire Extra Medium when your company does not need an enterprise program roster but does need a marketing chief: a senior leader inside your business owning the plan, with a studio executing behind that seat in one engagement.
