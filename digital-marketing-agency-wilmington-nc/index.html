Straight answers

What does a digital marketing agency do for a Wilmington business?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Wilmington, and digital is where a growing city picks its providers. Newcomers arrive with no word of mouth to go on, so they search, compare and read reviews. We run paid search, paid social, email and web that put you first in that process.

A city that chooses on screens

Two of Wilmington’s biggest audiences arrive pre-sold on searching. The newcomer, unpacking in Ogden or Leland, needs a dentist, a roofer, a gym and a brunch spot, and has no cousin to ask. The visitor, planning a Wrightsville week from Charlotte or Raleigh, builds the whole trip on a phone. Even lifelong locals switch to search the moment they need something new. The moment of choice in this market is a results page and a review profile, and digital marketing is the discipline of being the obvious answer in both.

The channels, and the job each one does

Paid search takes the intent that already exists: the near-me searches and the service-plus-city searches from people ready to book, buy or call. Paid social creates demand and can be aimed with local precision, by neighborhood and interest, from downtown to Mayfaire to the beach towns. Email turns first-timers into regulars, which is where the profit in hospitality and retail actually lives. Your website is where all of it lands, so it has to load fast, answer plainly and make the next step obvious on a phone. Retargeting ties the journey together for visitors who looked in March and book in May. Our services page covers the full digital mix.

Measured like owners

Budgets get sized from the value of a customer in your category, not from a package, and reporting runs against revenue, bookings and appointments in plain language. Channels that produce get fed; channels that do not get stopped, and we will tell you which is which. In a market growing this fast, the waste is not standing still. It is spending on activity nobody is judging.

Key takeaways

  • In a growing city, the search results page is the new Main Street. Be on it, or be skipped.
  • Newcomers and visitors choose on their phones. Locals do too, the moment they need something new.
  • Digital budgets should be sized from the value of a customer, not from a package.

Asked often.

We are at the beach. Is digital different for us?

Yes, in timing. Beach businesses sell to people planning from somewhere else, weeks ahead, and to locals in the off-season. Digital runs both: visitor campaigns during planning season, local offers when the crowds go home, on one budget that shifts with the calendar.

How much should a Wilmington business spend on digital ads?

Enough to be present in the searches that matter, and no more. We size budgets from the value of a customer and the cost of a click in your category, prove it in the first month, and adjust. Anyone who quotes a number before that math is guessing with your money.

How do we start?

In writing. Send a note through our contact page with what you sell and who buys it. You will get a specific reply on which channels we would run first and what it would cost.
