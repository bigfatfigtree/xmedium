Straight answers

What should a digital marketing agency in Nashville actually run for you?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Nashville, and our digital practice runs paid search, paid social, SEO, email and web as one system instead of five separate vendors. Every channel is planned against the same strategy and measured against revenue, not clicks. Senior people run it, start to finish.

One system, not five vendors

The typical Nashville digital setup is a patchwork: one freelancer on ads, a web firm on retainer, somebody’s nephew on social, and dashboards that each claim credit for the same customer. Nobody owns the funnel, so every channel gets optimized and the business does not. Extra Medium runs digital as one system under one strategy, the same model we use as a marketing agency in Nashville: paid search and paid social feed the site, the site feeds email, SEO compounds underneath all of it, and the reporting answers to revenue.

Built for a city people search before they visit

Nashville demand starts on a phone screen. Visitors plan trips months out, comparing hotels, venues, restaurants and experiences long before they land. Locals search at neighborhood level: a dentist in Green Hills, a contractor in The Nations, a clinic near the healthcare campuses that anchor the economy. New residents arrive weekly and search for everything from scratch. Digital built for this city has to capture the trip-planning search, the near-me search and the newcomer search at the same time, and most vendor patchworks only ever chase one of them.

Creators and AI, in the same system

Two parts of digital most Nashville agencies bolt on, we build in. Creator marketing is part of the plan from the start, because local voices move this city; our influencer marketing for local businesses page explains how that works at neighborhood scale. And our AI practice covers the newest channel of all: being found and quoted when people ask AI tools where to go, who to hire and what to book, plus research and content systems that make senior time go further.

Key takeaways

  • Five vendors optimizing five dashboards is not a digital strategy.
  • Nashville demand starts on a phone screen, from visitors planning trips and locals searching their own neighborhoods.
  • Channels get measured against revenue here, not clicks.

Asked often.

Which digital channels do you run?

Paid search, paid social, SEO, email, web and creator marketing, planned as one system. We will also tell you which channels not to fund yet. In a city with Nashville's visitor volume, the right mix depends on whether your customer is planning a trip, moving here, or already living three streets away.

How do we know what digital marketing will cost?

You get the engagement shape and price in writing before work begins, with the channels and cadence spelled out. No percentage-of-spend fog and no surprise line items. If a channel is not earning, the plan says so and the money moves.

Can you work with our in-house marketing person?

Yes, and it is a common shape here. The studio can execute behind an internal marketer who needs senior range, or a fractional CMO can lead them and own the strategy. Either way the hand-offs disappear, because the team sits in one company.
