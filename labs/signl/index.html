The problem

Influencer marketing skipped local.

A neighborhood restaurant doesn’t need a creator with two million followers in another state. It needs the 2,000 people within walking distance who actually decide where Friday night happens — and those people follow neighbors, not influencers.

Meanwhile local creators with small, fiercely local audiences had no front door: brands couldn’t find them, couldn’t book them properly, and couldn’t tell what a visit actually brought in.

So we built the front door — a two-sided platform where the standards are the marketing: published pricing, a two-business-day decision on every application, tracked visits, and a Visit Guarantee (a visit that doesn’t happen, or a post that never lands, doesn’t count and gets replaced).

What the studio built

Everything a launch takes. Ours.

Brand & naming — SIGNL, a one-word invented name with the gradient identity system, designed here.

Product & platform — creator applications with automatic tiering (Neighbors 500–2k, Locals 2k–15k, Headliners 15k+, stacked across platforms), business onboarding, offer → visit → post → tracked-outcome lifecycle.

Business model — two lanes: comped visits, and paid bookings where creators keep 97%. Published plans at $299 / $599 / $999 a month.

Go-to-market — city-by-city founding rosters, hand-picked, market by market — home market Virginia Beach first. Operations — run daily by this studio, under Extra Medium LLC.

How a visit works

Four steps, no theater.

01 · The offer

A business makes an offer

A comped dinner, a service, an experience — or a paid booking at the creator’s own rate.

02 · The visit

A local creator shows up

Someone from the neighborhood, not a stranger passing through. Their followers are your neighbors too.

03 · The post

They post it honestly

One post or reel plus two stories within 48 hours. Disclosed, in their own voice, never a script.

04 · The proof

The business sees what it brought in

Every visit tracked. Register spend counted at the till; media value modeled and labeled as modeled. Honest numbers, always.
