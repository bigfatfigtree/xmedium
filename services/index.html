Before you ask.

Studio questions
How much does it cost to work with Extra Medium?

It depends on the shape. Fractional leadership is a monthly engagement, projects are scoped with a clear price range after the first conversation, and ongoing growth work is a monthly system with one owner. We do not publish a rate card because the honest price follows the actual problem — the first conversation is free and specific.

How fast can a launch or rebrand ship?

Focused launches and rebrands typically run in weeks, not quarters, because one senior team owns strategy, creative and build together with no handoffs. Exact timing is set in the first conversation once the destination is clear.

Do you only work with Virginia Beach companies?

No. Extra Medium is headquartered in Virginia Beach, Virginia, with presence in Chicago and London, and clients across the US and UK. Most engagements run remotely with in-person time when it matters.

What makes you different from a typical agency?

Three things: every engagement is led personally by the founder; the team is senior-only with no account-manager telephone game; and the studio builds its own products in Extra Medium Labs — led by Magpie — so its advice is tested on its own money first.
