Straight answers

What does good social media marketing in Charlottesville look like?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Charlottesville, and we run social the way this town actually talks: through people. We handle social strategy, content and paid social, plus creator and influencer marketing through our own lab — including Magpie, our local creator platform — for brands built on a famous food scene and a small, fast grapevine.

A food scene bigger than the city

Charlottesville eats and drinks far above its weight, and social is where that reputation is maintained: the tasting room at golden hour, the Downtown Mall table everyone photographs, the chef whose specials sell out by story post. For restaurants, wineries, inns and shops, the feed is not advertising — it is the front window, checked by locals deciding on Friday and visitors building a weekend. We shoot and write for that window: real food, real rooms, real people, on a rhythm the business can actually sustain.

Creators, students and the local grapevine

In a small city, the influential voices are not celebrities. They are the local creator whose followers genuinely live here, the UVA student whose dorm-room recommendations move a weekend’s worth of brunch decisions, the regular whose post in a community group outperforms an ad. Our lab builds and runs Magpie, a local creator marketing platform, so we select creators by real local reach, shown before anyone books — not by follower counts that mean nothing thirty miles from the county line.

What we run, week to week

Strategy, content production, community management and paid social, run as one engagement from our services practice. The calendar follows the city: harvest and football in the fall, the quiet creativity of winter, graduation and wedding season in spring. Reporting counts what matters — reservations, bookings, ticket and list sign-ups — with follower growth kept in its place, as a supporting metric that only means something when the followers are the right people.

Key takeaways

  • In a small city, social media is just word of mouth with a screenshot.
  • One local creator who actually eats here beats a celebrity who flew over.
  • Your guests are already making your content. Give them a reason to tag it.

Asked often.

Do wineries and restaurants really need influencer marketing?

They need trusted voices, which is what creator marketing is when it is done locally. A tasting room visit from a creator whose audience plans Virginia weekends will outperform a national post almost every time, because her followers can actually come. The rule is fit and real local reach, not fame.

How often should a Charlottesville business post?

As often as it can post something worth a local’s attention — for most businesses, a few strong posts a week plus stories around real moments: a new vintage, a menu change, a market day. A daily quota of filler trains this small audience to ignore you, and in Charlottesville you cannot buy a new audience when the first one tunes out.

How do we start?

In writing. Send the problem through our contact page and you will get a specific reply: whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost. Nobody books a call on your calendar unless you ask for one.
