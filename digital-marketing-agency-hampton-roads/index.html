Straight answers

What does a digital marketing agency in Hampton Roads actually do for a local business?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Hampton Roads, and digital is where this region does its shopping, hiring and trip-planning. We run the full digital mix: SEO, paid search and paid social, social media, email, content and websites that convert, measured against revenue instead of impressions and likes.

The full mix, run as one system

Digital marketing fails in pieces. A business boosts a post here, runs a search ad there, sends an email when someone remembers, and wonders why the total feels like weather instead of climate. We run the channels as one system: SEO so you are found when people look, paid search and paid social to buy the demand you cannot wait for, social media to stay present between purchases, email to bring people back, content that gives all of it something worth saying, and a website built to turn the visit into a call, a booking or a sale. What each piece includes is laid out on our services page.

Built for how this region actually moves

Hampton Roads has rhythms most agencies never see. Every summer, thousands of military families arrive on orders and search for dentists, schools, gyms and realtors before the moving truck does. Tourists plan Oceanfront trips weeks out, on phones, comparing everything. Shipyard and defense employers recruit in a permanent labor shortage, and their candidates search like consumers. And locals commute across seven cities daily, so the customer in Norfolk at noon is the customer in Chesapeake by six. Digital campaigns here have to be timed and targeted for all four audiences at once, which is exactly how we build them.

Measured like owners, not vendors

Every engagement reports against revenue: calls, bookings, sales and pipeline, traced back to the channel that produced them. Activity metrics are fine for a dashboard and useless for a decision. If a channel is producing reports instead of customers, we say so and move the money — the same standard we run on our own products, and the same one behind our fractional CMO work when a company needs the strategy seat filled above the channels.

Key takeaways

  • In Hampton Roads, your next customer is often searching from another city, another state, or a ship headed home.
  • Digital channels work as a system or not at all: search catches intent, social creates it, email keeps it.
  • Impressions are weather. Revenue is climate. We report on climate.

Asked often.

Which digital channel should a Hampton Roads business start with?

Whichever one sits closest to the money. If people already search for what you sell, start with search: SEO and paid search catch existing demand. If your offer needs explaining or showing, social and content usually lead. We sequence channels by payback, not by what is fashionable.

Do you rebuild websites, or only market them?

Both. A slow or confusing website taxes every other channel, so web design and conversion work are part of the practice. Sometimes that is a rebuild; often it is fixing the five pages that carry most of the traffic.

How do we start?

Send a note through the contact page with what you sell and where it is stuck. We reply in writing with what we would look at first and what an engagement would look like. No pressure, no phone tag.
