What Taoti Creative does

Taoti Creative is an independent, full-service creative agency on Capitol Hill in Washington, established in 1996. Its promise is to move the needle: meaningful, measurable results across web, social, experiential and technical work.

Its services run from web design and development on Drupal and WordPress to digital marketing, social media management, branding, experiential marketing, video and TV commercials, podcasts, AR/VR, SEO, UX design, accessibility and event production. The firm built the redesigned DC.gov, and its own pages name clients including the World Bank, USAID, DC Water, Discovery Education, the American Chemical Society and the Jane Goodall Foundation.

Where Extra Medium is different

The difference is what you are hiring. Taoti gives you a project team: you brief a website, a campaign or an experience, and they build it. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Washington, DC built around a seat. A named senior operator sits in your leadership meetings as your fractional CMO in Washington, DC and owns positioning, the plan and the budget.

Behind that seat, the studio executes: brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid media, social, influencer and PR. The team is founder-led and senior-only, so the person in your meeting is the person doing the thinking, and the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

The part most agencies cannot do

Then there is the building. Extra Medium runs a lab that ships products, not just campaigns: a live creator marketplace (Magpie) and a live platform for ticketed celebrity sessions (Hapsta) were both designed, built and launched by this team. If growth needs a platform under it, a product, an app, a tool your customers log into, you do not have to hire a second firm and teach it your business.

Its Intelligence practice does the same on the AI side: getting companies cited by AI search, building research and content systems, and deploying AI assistants with a hard privacy rule, a company’s data answers only its own questions. The operator in the seat has run digital strategy for media companies and influencer marketing at scale for large brands, so the advice comes from having shipped it.

Who should hire which

Hire Taoti Creative if your need is a build: a major website on Drupal or WordPress, a campaign, an experiential program or a video production, delivered by an experienced Capitol Hill team that has done it for national associations, government clients and institutions since 1996.

Hire Extra Medium if the need is ongoing leadership rather than a project: a senior operator inside your team who owns the marketing plan across every channel, quarter after quarter, with the studio executing behind the seat and the price agreed in writing first.

Asked often.

Is Extra Medium a good alternative to Taoti Creative?

It depends on what you are missing. If you need a website, campaign or experience built by a veteran project team, Taoti Creative is a strong, established choice and you should talk to them. If the gap is senior marketing leadership, a CMO-level operator who owns positioning, plan and budget inside your business, that is the seat Extra Medium sells, with a studio behind it. The two firms are different models, not better and worse versions of the same thing.

Does Taoti Creative publish its pricing?

No. Checked in November 2026, Taoti Creative does not publish prices, rates or package costs on its website; like most agencies, it quotes work privately once it understands the scope. Extra Medium does not post a public rate card either. The difference is procedural: before any work begins, you get the engagement shape and price in writing, so the commitment is clear before you make it.

How does working with Extra Medium start?

With a first conversation that is free and specific: your market, your current marketing, and what is actually stuck. If the fractional CMO seat fits, you receive the engagement shape and price in writing before any work begins. If your real need is smaller than the seat, we will tell you that instead, and point you at what would actually help.
