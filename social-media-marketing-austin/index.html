Straight answers

Looking for social media marketing in Austin?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Austin, and social is one of the main ways this city decides what to eat, hear and buy. We plan and run content, creators and community as part of one strategy, not a posting calendar. Senior people, honest metrics, no filler.

A city that lives out loud

Austin’s culture is its media. Live music every night, food trucks with lines around the block, festivals that take over downtown, and a population that documents all of it as it happens. Social media is where this city decides what is worth its time, and brands are welcome in that conversation only when they add something real. We plan social as part of the wider work of a marketing agency in Austin: rooted in the city’s actual life, not a content calendar copied from a template.

Creators who actually move Austin

The voices Austin trusts are local: the creator who knows every taco truck on the east side moves more people than a national spot does. Extra Medium has run influencer marketing at scale for large brands, and our Labs arm built Magpie, a platform that ranks local creators by the share of their audience that actually lives in the market. That combination means creator programs here start from data about real local reach, not follower counts, and get managed like media, with briefs, deadlines and honest reporting. More on the model is on our influencer marketing for local businesses page.

Planned like marketing, not chores

Posting is the smallest part of the job. The real work is deciding what your brand stands for in this city’s feed, producing content worth stopping for, managing the community that answers back, and supporting the winners with paid social. We report against outcomes a business can feel: reach among Austin buyers, visits, bookings and sales, reviewed on a cadence with the person who owns your strategy. No filler posts, no bought followers, no vanity dashboards.

Asked often.

Which platforms matter most for Austin businesses?

It depends on the customer. Instagram and TikTok drive food, music, retail and lifestyle decisions in Austin; LinkedIn carries the tech and B2B conversation; Facebook still matters for community groups and older buyers. We recommend the mix your audience actually uses and tell you which to skip.

Do you work with Austin creators and influencers?

Yes. Creator programs are a core part of the practice, built on local reach data rather than follower counts. We handle selection, briefs, deliverables and disclosure, and we measure what the program produced.

How often should an Austin business post?

As often as you can post something worth seeing, and no oftener. For most local brands that means a few strong posts a week plus stories and community replies, planned monthly and adjusted around the city's calendar, from festival season to the holidays.
