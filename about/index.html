About Extra Medium

A studio for businesses becoming who they were meant to be.

Most agencies will tell you what they do. We’d rather talk about where you’re going. But since you asked — here is who we are, and why it works.

Founder & Principal

Jeff Rimmer

More than twenty years in marketing, on every side of the table. Inside startups and inside Fortune 100 companies. Founder of several businesses of his own — including a music platform taken from a blank page to hundreds of thousands of users, and now Magpie, built in our own Labs. And the outside partner to clients across that same range, from a founder with a napkin sketch to enterprise teams serving millions.

That mix is the point. Jeff can write the positioning, direct the creative, build the growth engine and wire the intelligence underneath it — and has done each of those jobs for real, with his own budget on the line. Deepest experience: music and entertainment, retail and ecommerce, healthcare and wellness — three industries where attention is expensive and trust is everything.

He founded Extra Medium to give ambitious businesses one senior partner who thinks like an owner, builds like a studio and measures like an operator. He leads every engagement personally, works from Virginia Beach, and travels regularly to Chicago and the UK.

A word about the name

Medium is a compliment nobody wants.

We believe boring is a choice, and we don’t make it. We believe extra isn’t too much — it’s the whole point. And great businesses are built when someone connects every medium together: brand and growth, story and intelligence, leadership and experience. The medium changes. The idea doesn’t.
