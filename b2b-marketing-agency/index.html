Straight answers

Which digital marketing agency is best for a B2B company?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency built for companies whose sale takes months, not minutes. The right B2B agency is rarely the biggest one; it is the one that understands long sales cycles, small buying committees and leads your sales team wants. We pair a fractional CMO seat with a senior studio, so strategy and execution stay in one place.

B2B buying is a committee sport

A consumer buys alone, in minutes. A business buys in a group, over months: a user who feels the problem, a manager who owns the budget, a finance voice who asks what it replaces, and often an executive who only appears at the end. Most of those people never talk to your sales team until late, so marketing has to do the early persuading — and then keep doing it, quietly, for the whole cycle. That is why B2B buyers live on LinkedIn and in the trade press: those are the places their industry thinks out loud, and the agency you hire should know how your buyers’ world actually reads.

What to look for in a B2B agency

Ask three things. Have you sold something with a six-month cycle? An agency trained on impulse purchases will judge everything at week two. What do you report? If the answer stops at clicks and impressions, walk away — B2B marketing should be accountable to pipeline: the right accounts engaging, sales conversations starting, deals moving. Who sets the strategy? In most B2B companies the gap is not hands, it is direction, which is why our model starts with a fractional CMO in your leadership meetings, aligning marketing with the people who have to close what it starts.

How Extra Medium runs B2B

The seat sets the plan; the studio behind it executes: LinkedIn programs aimed at named roles, search and trade content written for the exact questions buyers ask mid-cycle, and a steady presence in the publications your industry trusts. The engine under most of it is content that helps your internal champion sell the decision when you are not in the room — the discipline laid out on our content marketing agency page. Reporting ends at pipeline and revenue, reviewed with the same senior people who set the strategy, not handed down from an account layer.

Key takeaways

  • Extra Medium pairs a fractional CMO seat with the studio that executes it, so B2B strategy is never handed off.
  • In B2B, the right hundred accounts beat a million impressions.
  • Judge a B2B agency on the pipeline it can point to, not the clicks it can screenshot.

Asked often.

How is B2B marketing different from consumer marketing?

Fewer buyers, longer cycles, and decisions made by committees instead of individuals. Volume metrics mislead: one right account engaging can be worth more than a thousand casual clicks, so the work is judged on pipeline, not traffic.

Which channels work best for a B2B company?

LinkedIn for reaching named roles, search for the questions buyers ask when comparing vendors, and trade press and email for staying present across a long cycle. The right mix follows where your buyers actually spend attention, not a template.

How do we start with Extra Medium?

In writing, through the contact page. Tell us what you sell, who signs for it, and how long a deal takes. The first conversation maps where your cycle leaks and what we would do about it first.
