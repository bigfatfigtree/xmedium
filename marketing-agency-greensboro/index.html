Straight answers

Looking for a marketing agency in Greensboro that understands the Triad?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Greensboro, North Carolina, built for the Triad’s practical, value-conscious way of doing business. We run the full mix — strategy, digital, SEO, paid media, social, influencer and creative — with senior people doing the work, for companies that measure marketing by what it returns.

The full mix, one team

Strategy, digital marketing, SEO, paid search and paid social, social media, influencer and creator marketing, content, creative and branding, web design, email and fractional CMO leadership — run by one senior team instead of a relay of vendors. That is the studio: the person who sets the strategy is in the room when the work ships, and every channel answers to one plan and one set of numbers.

Triad companies usually do not have a channel problem. They have a coordination problem — a website from one vendor, ads from another, social from somebody’s nephew — and nobody senior joining it up. One team is the fix.

Marketing in the Triad

This region sells in particular ways. Furniture and home companies live by the High Point Market calendar and the long research cycles around it. Logistics and distribution, at the crossroads of I-40 and I-85, compete as much for people as for customers. Manufacturers carry decades of reputation into buyers who now research online first. And Greensboro’s universities, from UNCG to NC A&T, keep the market younger and more digital than its size suggests. Marketing that ignores those rhythms is just noise with a budget. When the missing piece is leadership, our fractional CMO practice fills the seat.

How an engagement starts

One conversation. Bring the problem as you see it — flat sales, a trade cycle you keep missing, marketing spend nobody can explain — and we will respond with whether we can help, what we would do first, and what it would cost, in writing, before any work begins. Practical people, plain numbers: the way this region prefers to do business anyway.

Key takeaways

  • Triad businesses buy on value. Marketing here has to show its math.
  • One senior team for strategy, creative and every channel. No hand-offs, no fluff.
  • From High Point Market cycles to year-round logistics growth, the plan fits how this region actually sells.

Asked often.

What kinds of Greensboro companies do you work with?

Manufacturers, logistics and distribution firms, furniture and home companies, professional services, healthcare and growth-stage local businesses. The common thread is a leadership team that wants senior marketing without building a full department first.

Do you work across the whole Triad?

Yes. Greensboro, Winston-Salem and High Point function as one market for most buyers, and we plan that way — while keeping each city’s character in the creative, because locals can tell the difference.

How do we start?

Start on the contact page with a few lines about the company and the problem. A human replies within one business day, in writing, with next steps. No call is required to get a straight answer.
