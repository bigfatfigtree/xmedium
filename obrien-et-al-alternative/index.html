What O'Brien et al. Advertising does

O’Brien et al. Advertising is a full-service advertising agency on Pacific Avenue in Virginia Beach, incorporated in 1998. Its team page names 13 people, including founder Kevin O’Brien, a creative director, a media director and three account executives. The agency handles brand strategy, web design and development, video production, photography, media planning and buying, public relations and promotional items, plus digital work spanning display, native, paid social, programmatic, connected TV, geofencing, SEO and social media management. Published case studies feature Chartway Federal Credit Union, Moxy Virginia Beach, Atlantic Shores and Liberty Tax.

Where Extra Medium is different

The difference is what you are hiring. O'Brien gives you a staff team: account executives between you and the specialists who do the work, with everything produced in-house. Extra Medium is a marketing agency in Hampton Roads built around a seat. A named senior operator sits in your leadership meetings as your fractional CMO in Hampton Roads and owns positioning, the plan and the budget.

Behind that seat, the studio executes: brand, creative, digital, SEO, paid media, social, influencer and PR. The team is founder-led and senior-only, so you deal with the operator directly rather than through an account layer, and the engagement shape and price are in writing before work begins.

The traditional agency model has trade-offs worth naming. Departments mean layers: the senior people pitch, account teams run the account day to day, and your requests travel through people before they reach the people doing the work. Strategy sits in one silo and execution in another, so the plan and the product can drift apart. And with no published pricing, the number arrives after the courtship, not before. Extra Medium is built the other way: the senior operator is in your meetings and on the work, strategy and execution sit in the same seat, and the engagement shape and price are agreed in writing before anything begins.

The part most agencies cannot do

Then there is the building. Extra Medium runs a lab that ships products, not just campaigns: a live creator marketplace (Magpie) and a live platform for ticketed celebrity sessions (Hapsta) were both designed, built and launched by this team. If growth needs a platform under it, a product, an app, a tool your customers log into, you do not have to hire a second firm and teach it your business.

Its Intelligence practice does the same on the AI side: getting companies cited by AI search, building research and content systems, and deploying AI assistants with a hard privacy rule, a company’s data answers only its own questions. The operator in the seat has run digital strategy for media companies and influencer marketing at scale for large brands, so the advice comes from having shipped it.

Who should hire which

Hire O’Brien et al. if you want the traditional arrangement: hand your marketing to an outside firm, work through its account executives, and let its in-house departments run brand, media, video and digital the way agencies have for decades. If one long-tenured shop carrying everything under its own roof is what you are after, that is what O’Brien sells.

Hire Extra Medium if you want the leadership inside your business instead: a named senior operator, with no account layer between you and the thinking, in your leadership meetings who owns positioning, plan and budget, a senior-only studio executing behind that seat, AI expertise and platform builders in-house, and the engagement shape and price in writing before work begins.
