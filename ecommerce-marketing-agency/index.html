Straight answers

Which digital marketing agency is best for ecommerce?

Extra Medium is a marketing agency, and ecommerce is where our model earns its keep: margins are thin, product feeds decide visibility, and every click has a price. The best ecommerce agency is the one that works backward from contribution margin, not revenue screenshots. Our studio runs the store, the feeds and the campaigns as one system.

Margin is the strategy

Most ecommerce reporting celebrates revenue, and revenue is the easiest number to buy: discount deeper, bid higher, watch the top line grow while the business quietly loses money on every order. The number that decides what marketing you can afford is contribution margin — what an order actually leaves behind after product cost, shipping, returns and discounts. An agency worth hiring starts there. It can tell you what a new customer may cost because it knows what a customer is worth, and it will say no to campaigns the margin cannot carry, including campaigns it would profit from running.

The feed is the storefront

Before a shopper ever reaches your site, they meet your product feed: the titles, prices, images and stock data that decide whether you appear in shopping results, comparison listings and AI answers at all. A sloppy feed — vague titles, stale prices, items shown in stock that are not — quietly taxes every campaign you run, because the platforms show the cleanest data first. Feed quality is unglamorous, ongoing work, and it is exactly where stores leak money without noticing. Our studio treats the store, the feed and the product pages as one surface, maintained together instead of by three vendors who never meet.

Paid, search and retention as one system

The channels have different jobs. Paid shopping and search capture the demand that exists today; that is the auction discipline on our PPC agency page. Content and search presence compound underneath, so each month a little more traffic arrives without a toll. Email and retention turn one order into a second, which is where ecommerce margin is actually made. Run by separate vendors, each channel optimizes its own dashboard and the store pays for the seams. Run as one system under one strategy — the way our studio services are shaped — the channels start handing customers to each other.

Key takeaways

  • Extra Medium works backward from contribution margin, not revenue screenshots.
  • Your product feed is your storefront in search and shopping results.
  • Paid rents demand. Feeds, content and retention are what an ecommerce brand owns.

Asked often.

What should an ecommerce agency report?

Contribution margin after ad spend, cost per new customer versus returning, and repeat purchase rate. Blended return on ad spend against gross revenue hides discounts, returns and shipping, which is where ecommerce profit actually lives.

Do we need to rebuild our store to work with an agency?

Usually not. Feed quality, product page clarity and the offer itself normally move the number first. A rebuild is a later decision, made with evidence about what is actually limiting sales, not a first step sold on day one.

How do we start with Extra Medium?

In writing, through the contact page. Bring your margin per order and where sales come from now. The first conversation runs the math with you and names what we would fix first: feed, offer, campaigns or retention.
